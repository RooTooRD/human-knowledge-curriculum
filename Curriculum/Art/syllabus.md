# Art Syllabus

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ART-B01` Seeing and describing visual elements | `FND-B03`, `FND-B09` | 1. Line/shape; 2. color/value; 3. texture/material; 4. space/form; 5. composition/emphasis | Describe without premature judgment; analyze formal relationships; compare visual effects | “I like it” is a response, not yet analysis | ☐ Formal analysis of unseen work; ☐ recreate composition changes |
| `ART-B02` Drawing, observation, proportion, perspective, light, iteration | `ART-B01`, `MAT-B07` | 1. Contour/gesture; 2. proportion/negative space; 3. perspective; 4. light/value; 5. studies/revision | Draw observed form; construct space/light; iterate from critique | Drawing skill is trainable perception and coordination, not only innate talent | ☐ Observational portfolio; ☐ documented revision |
| `ART-B03` Materials and processes | `ART-B01`, `FND-B08` | 1. Painting/pigment; 2. print; 3. sculpture/craft; 4. photography/video; 5. digital/time media and safety | Select/process materials; explain affordances; document safe workflow | Medium is not neutral container; it shapes meaning and production | ☐ Multi-medium studies; ☐ process/safety defense |
| `ART-I01` Representation, abstraction, symbol, iconography, style, patronage, audience | `ART-B01`, `LIT-B01` | 1. Mimesis/abstraction; 2. symbol/icon; 3. style/convention; 4. patron/function; 5. audience/reception | Interpret visual conventions; connect form/function; separate iconography from speculation | Realistic appearance is not necessarily greater truth or skill | ☐ Contextual object analysis; ☐ compare representation systems |
| `ART-I02` Prehistoric, ancient, classical, African, Asian, Indigenous, American arts | `ART-I01`, `HST-B05` | 1. Prehistoric/material; 2. Africa/West Asia; 3. South/East Asia; 4. Mediterranean; 5. Indigenous Americas/Oceania | Identify major traditions/context; compare functions/materials; avoid universal hierarchy | “Primitive” is not a valid measure of artistic sophistication | ☐ Comparative visual exam; ☐ curate global thematic set |
| `ART-I03` Islamic, Byzantine, medieval, connected early modern arts | `ART-I02`, `HST-I01` | 1. Byzantine/Christian; 2. Islamic architecture/object/book; 3. African/Asian connections; 4. medieval Europe; 5. exchange/pilgrimage/court | Analyze sacred/courtly forms; trace exchange; interpret ornament/material | Islamic art is not uniformly nonfigural or reducible to geometry | ☐ Connected-object study; ☐ visual-source comparison |
| `ART-I04` Renaissance, early modern, colonial, global exchange | `ART-I03`, `HST-I02` | 1. Renaissance workshops/perspective; 2. courts/reformations; 3. Mughal/Ottoman/Safavid; 4. colonial exchange; 5. collecting/academies | Compare early modern centers; analyze patronage/coloniality; trace objects | Renaissance was neither sudden rebirth nor isolated European achievement | ☐ Comparative exhibition plan; ☐ provenance route analysis |
| `ART-I05` Modernism, photography, design, mass culture, global modernities | `ART-I04`, `HST-I04` | 1. Revolution/realism; 2. photography; 3. avant-gardes; 4. design/mass media; 5. global/colonial modernisms | Analyze formal rupture/institution; compare modernisms; connect reproduction/audience | Modern art is not simply abandonment of skill | ☐ Movement comparison; ☐ medium/institution essay |
| `ART-I06` Contemporary art, installation, performance, digital media, institutions, markets | `ART-I05`, `HST-I06` | 1. Concept/installation; 2. performance/body; 3. video/digital/network; 4. identity/global biennials; 5. museum/gallery/market | Interpret concept/context/material; analyze institutions/markets; document ephemeral work | Price and institutional validation do not settle artistic value | ☐ Contemporary work dossier; ☐ institution/market critique |
| `ART-I07` Criticism, aesthetics, interpretation, conservation, museums, provenance, restitution | `ART-I01`, `PHI-I07`, `HST-A02` | 1. Description/interpretation/judgment; 2. aesthetic theory; 3. conservation/material change; 4. museum/curation; 5. provenance/restitution | Write reasoned criticism; assess conservation; research ownership/return | Conservation is not restoring an object to one uncontested original state | ☐ Critical review; ☐ provenance/restitution case |
| `ART-A01` Studio practice | `ART-B02`, `ART-B03`, `ART-I07` | 1. Question/research; 2. material experiments; 3. series/development; 4. critique/revision; 5. portfolio/exhibition/statement | Develop coherent body of work; use critique; install/document professionally | Artist statement cannot supply meaning absent from the work's decisions | ☐ Exhibit resolved series; ☐ defend process/revision |
| `ART-A02` Visual culture, media, politics, identity, religion, science, technology | `ART-I06`, `COM-I05`, `SOC-I04` | 1. Image/power; 2. advertising/media; 3. race/gender/class; 4. sacred/scientific image; 5. surveillance/AI/memes | Analyze images in social systems; trace circulation; evaluate representation ethics | Images do not merely reflect society; they participate in making categories/action | ☐ Visual-culture study; ☐ ethical image intervention |
| `ART-A03` Curatorial, archival, conservation, public-art practice | `ART-I07`, `RSH-I05` | 1. Curatorial thesis; 2. selection/research; 3. loans/rights/conservation; 4. interpretation/access; 5. public art/community/evaluation | Curate coherent accessible exhibition; manage objects/rights; collaborate publicly | Curation is argument and care, not neutral arrangement | ☐ Deliver exhibition/public project; ☐ stakeholder/conservation review |
| `ART-E01` Original artistic or art-historical research | `ART-A01`, `ART-A02`, `RSH-E01` | 1. Original question/practice; 2. archive/material; 3. sustained production/argument; 4. critique/context; 5. exhibition/publication | Produce original artistic/scholarly contribution; situate it critically; document responsibly | Novel appearance alone is not sustained inquiry | ☐ Major exhibition/thesis; ☐ pass expert/public defense |
