# Health and Medicine Syllabus

> [!CAUTION]
> Mastery here supports informed decisions and citizenship; it does not authorize
> diagnosis, prescribing, or clinical practice.

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `HLT-B01` Health, disease, disability, functioning, prevention, systems | `BIO-B05`, `FND-I05` | 1. Health models; 2. disease/illness/disability; 3. risk/protective factors; 4. prevention levels; 5. healthcare/public-health systems | Distinguish concepts; locate appropriate care/prevention; explain social determinants | Health is not merely absence of diagnosed disease | ☐ Map a health pathway; ☐ analyze determinants/prevention |
| `HLT-B02` Anatomy and physiology by organ system | `HLT-B01`, `BIO-I03` | 1. Anatomical language/tissues; 2. nervous/endocrine; 3. cardiovascular/respiratory; 4. digestive/renal; 5. musculoskeletal/immune/reproductive integration | Locate structures; explain system functions/feedback; predict basic dysfunction | Organ systems are interdependent, not isolated machines | ☐ Complete integrated case map; ☐ explain homeostatic response |
| `HLT-B03` Nutrition, movement, sleep, stress, substances, sexual health | `HLT-B01`, `CHM-A02` | 1. Energy/nutrients/eating patterns; 2. activity/fitness/sedentary risk; 3. sleep/circadian health; 4. stress/substances; 5. sexual/reproductive health and prevention | Evaluate lifestyle evidence; make sustainable risk-reduction plan; recognize help thresholds | Single foods/supplements rarely dominate overall health pattern | ☐ Audit one-week behavior safely; ☐ critique a health claim |
| `HLT-B04` First aid, emergencies, warning signs, help-seeking | `HLT-B01`, `FND-I04` | 1. Scene safety/assessment; 2. emergency activation/CPR orientation; 3. bleeding/burns/injury; 4. poisoning/allergy/heat/cold; 5. red flags and handoff | Respond within training; activate help; give concise handoff | First aid supports but does not replace professional emergency care | ☐ Pass certified practical where available; ☐ complete scenarios |
| `HLT-I01` Pathology, infection, immunity, injury, genetics, chronic disease | `HLT-B02`, `BIO-I06` | 1. Cell injury/inflammation; 2. infection/immunity; 3. neoplasia; 4. genetic/metabolic disease; 5. chronic multimorbidity | Explain major disease mechanisms; connect pathology to signs; distinguish risk/cause | A risk factor is not destiny or proof of individual causation | ☐ Build mechanism map for cases; ☐ interpret basic pathology evidence |
| `HLT-I02` Diagnosis, screening, prognosis, treatment, rehabilitation, palliation | `HLT-I01`, `STA-I03` | 1. History/exam/test; 2. differential reasoning; 3. sensitivity/specificity/predictive value; 4. treatment benefit/harm; 5. rehab/palliative goals | Interpret tests with base rates; compare options; participate in shared decisions | Earlier detection is not always beneficial when overdiagnosis exists | ☐ Solve diagnostic-risk cases; ☐ create balanced decision aid |
| `HLT-I03` Medicines, vaccines, devices, benefits, harms, interactions, adherence | `HLT-I02`, `CHM-I03` | 1. Drug targets/dose/route; 2. kinetics/dynamics; 3. trials/labels/adverse effects; 4. vaccines/devices; 5. interactions, adherence, reporting | Read medication information; compare absolute benefit/harm; identify interaction questions | “Natural” does not mean safe, and more medication is not always better | ☐ Audit a hypothetical regimen; ☐ explain vaccine/medicine evidence |
| `HLT-I04` Mental health, distress, disorder, crisis, therapy, recovery | `HLT-B01`, `PSY-I05` | 1. Well-being/distress/disorder; 2. major symptom patterns; 3. crisis/suicide warning and referral; 4. therapy/medication/social care; 5. stigma/recovery/support | Recognize concern and escalation; discuss without diagnosis/stigma; compare evidence-based supports | Ordinary distress is not automatically disorder, while severe symptoms are not moral weakness | ☐ Respond to crisis scenarios; ☐ produce support/referral plan |
| `HLT-I05` Evidence-based medicine and shared decisions | `HLT-I02`, `RSH-I04`, `STA-I03` | 1. Clinical question; 2. trial/design hierarchy; 3. relative/absolute effects and NNT/NNH; 4. guidelines/certainty; 5. values/shared decision | Appraise clinical evidence; calculate absolute effects; integrate evidence and preference | “Statistically significant” does not mean clinically important | ☐ Critically appraise a trial; ☐ conduct simulated shared decision |
| `HLT-I06` Public health | `HLT-I01`, `STA-I04`, `SOC-I03` | 1. Population measures; 2. surveillance/epidemiology; 3. prevention/promotion; 4. environmental/occupational/social health; 5. programs/communication | Calculate/interpret population measures; design prevention; evaluate program equity | Population association does not automatically dictate individual treatment | ☐ Analyze surveillance data; ☐ design evaluated intervention |
| `HLT-A01` Outbreaks, global health, one health, health security | `HLT-I06`, `BIO-I06`, `ECS-I07` | 1. Outbreak detection; 2. transmission/control; 3. field investigation; 4. animal-environment-human interfaces; 5. international response/equity | Investigate outbreak; compare controls; coordinate one-health response | Border closure alone rarely substitutes for layered control | ☐ Complete outbreak simulation; ☐ defend proportional response |
| `HLT-I07` Healthcare organization, access, quality, safety, financing, inequality | `HLT-B01`, `ECO-I03`, `POL-I04` | 1. Delivery models; 2. workforce/access; 3. quality/safety; 4. insurance/payment; 5. inequality/reform | Compare systems; analyze incentives; identify safety/access failures | Higher spending does not necessarily mean better population outcomes | ☐ Produce comparative system brief; ☐ redesign one care pathway |
| `HLT-A02` Medical ethics | `HLT-I05`, `PHI-A02`, `LAW-I05` | 1. Autonomy/capacity/consent; 2. benefit/nonharm; 3. confidentiality; 4. allocation/public health; 5. end-of-life/research ethics | Analyze cases; balance principles/context; distinguish ethical/legal questions | Respecting autonomy does not mean granting every requested intervention | ☐ Write ethics consultation; ☐ defend allocation/end-life reasoning |
| `HLT-A03` Critical appraisal of guidelines, claims, tests, and media | `HLT-I05`, `COM-I06`, `RSH-A03` | 1. Claim/source/conflict; 2. diagnostic/screening evidence; 3. review/guideline methods; 4. media exaggeration; 5. uncertainty/correction | Audit health claims; compare guidelines; communicate calibrated conclusion | Consensus can be strong evidence without being infallible | ☐ Publish evidence brief; ☐ correct misleading report |
| `HLT-A04` Genomic, digital, AI-assisted, and precision medicine | `HLT-I03`, `BIO-A02`, `AIX-A05` | 1. Genomic risk/tests; 2. biomarkers/stratification; 3. wearables/digital health; 4. clinical AI; 5. privacy, bias, validation, governance | Interpret genomic/AI performance; assess clinical utility; design oversight | Better prediction does not necessarily improve outcomes | ☐ Evaluate a precision tool; ☐ construct clinical safety case |
| `HLT-E01` Health research and policy synthesis | `HLT-A01`, `HLT-A02`, `RSH-E01` | 1. Burden/equity question; 2. evidence synthesis; 3. intervention/economic/legal analysis; 4. implementation/evaluation; 5. policy/public communication | Produce actionable health policy research; integrate ethics/evidence/feasibility | Evidence alone cannot choose policy without values and institutional constraints | ☐ Deliver policy dossier; ☐ defend before technical/public panels |
