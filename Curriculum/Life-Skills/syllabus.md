# Life Skills Syllabus

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `LIF-B01` Values, roles, priorities, goals, routines, review | `FND-B04`, `LRN-B01` | 1. Values/evidence; 2. roles/seasons; 3. priorities/trade-offs; 4. goals/routines; 5. weekly/annual review | Build coherent priority system; translate values into calendar/budget; revise by evidence | Stated values are not priorities unless reflected in repeated choices | ☐ Complete life audit/plan; ☐ show one review-driven change |
| `LIF-B02` Time, attention, commitments, habits, rest, productivity | `LIF-B01`, `LRN-B02` | 1. Time inventory; 2. commitment capture; 3. calendar/task systems; 4. focus/habits; 5. rest/boundaries | Operate trusted planning system; estimate capacity; protect recovery | Productivity is not maximizing occupied hours | ☐ Run system four weeks; ☐ meet commitments without chronic overload |
| `LIF-B03` Household systems | `LIF-B01`, `FND-I02` | 1. Food safety/cooking; 2. cleaning/laundry; 3. utilities/appliances; 4. maintenance/tools; 5. records/supplies | Maintain safe functional home; prepare basic meals; schedule preventive maintenance | Waiting for failure is often costlier than routine maintenance | ☐ Complete household practical; ☐ create maintenance calendar |
| `LIF-B04` First aid, fire, weather, evacuation, contacts, supplies, plans | `FND-I04`, `HLT-B04` | 1. Local hazards; 2. alerts/contacts; 3. fire/medical response; 4. evacuation/shelter; 5. supplies/drills | Build and execute emergency plan; maintain supplies; communicate reunification | Stockpiling without plans, skills, rotation, and community is not preparedness | ☐ Conduct household drill; ☐ pass supply/contact audit |
| `LIF-B05` Digital life | `FND-B08`, `SEC-B02` | 1. File/naming/archive; 2. backup/recovery; 3. identity/privacy; 4. reputation/communication; 5. attention/media boundaries | Maintain recoverable digital records; secure identity; manage attention | Cloud sync is not a complete backup | ☐ Restore critical files; ☐ complete privacy/attention audit |
| `LIF-I01` Relationships | `COM-B01`, `PSY-I04` | 1. Empathy/curiosity; 2. attachment/trust; 3. consent/boundaries; 4. conflict/apology/repair; 5. friendship/family/community | Communicate needs/boundaries; repair harm; recognize unsafe patterns | Love or good intent does not cancel boundaries or impact | ☐ Conduct difficult conversation; ☐ build support/repair plan |
| `LIF-I02` Personal decisions | `PSY-I03`, `SYS-I06` | 1. Decision frame; 2. options/base rates; 3. uncertainty/value; 4. bias/precommitment; 5. review/learning | Make documented decisions; use outside view; distinguish reversible/irreversible | More analysis can become avoidance when information value is low | ☐ Complete decision journal; ☐ audit outcome/process separately |
| `LIF-I03` Personal finance, consumer judgment, contracts, insurance, planning | `FIN-B05`, `LAW-B03` | 1. Net worth/cash flow; 2. credit/debt; 3. insurance; 4. contract/consumer rights; 5. tax/estate/long-term plan | Maintain solvent plan; compare contracts; protect dependents/records | Income alone does not create financial resilience | ☐ Complete annual financial review; ☐ solve major-purchase scenario |
| `LIF-I04` Career | `LIF-B01`, `COM-I07`, `BUS-I01` | 1. Strength/evidence/interests; 2. labor-market exploration; 3. portfolio/CV/application; 4. interview/negotiation; 5. performance/network/transition | Test career hypotheses; present evidence; negotiate and transition ethically | A career is not a single irreversible identity choice | ☐ Complete informational/application cycle; ☐ build transition plan |
| `LIF-I05` Civic participation | `FND-I05`, `POL-B02`, `COM-I02` | 1. Jurisdictions/representatives; 2. voting/information; 3. meetings/deliberation; 4. service/association/organizing; 5. petition/oversight/local action | Navigate local institutions; deliberate from evidence; organize lawful action | Citizenship is more than periodic voting or online expression | ☐ Attend/analyze civic process; ☐ complete bounded community action |
| `LIF-I06` Leadership | `COM-A03`, `BUS-I02` | 1. Service/legitimacy; 2. vision/goals; 3. trust/delegation; 4. meeting/feedback/conflict; 5. succession/accountability | Lead collaborative work; delegate and develop others; build succession | Leadership is a practice/relationship, not title or charisma | ☐ Lead completed team project; ☐ collect/act on 360 feedback |
| `LIF-I07` Practical making, tools, repair, energy, transport, technology judgment | `LIF-B03`, `ENG-B02`, `ECS-I05` | 1. Tool/safety/material; 2. diagnose/repair; 3. electrical/plumbing orientation; 4. transport/energy choices; 5. repair-replace assessment | Diagnose before replacing; use tools safely; calculate lifecycle choice | Newer technology is not automatically more reliable or sustainable | ☐ Complete safe repair/build; ☐ document lifecycle decision |
| `LIF-A01` Caregiving, parenting, aging, grief, disability, end of life | `LIF-I01`, `HLT-I02`, `LAW-I04` | 1. Care relationship/burden; 2. child development/parenting; 3. disability/accommodation; 4. aging/grief; 5. directives/estate/end-life conversation | Plan respectful care; support autonomy; prepare legal/medical wishes | Caregiving should not erase the caregiver or recipient's agency | ☐ Create care/directive dossier; ☐ conduct simulated values conversation |
| `LIF-A02` Crisis judgment, resilience, recovery, mutual aid, preparedness | `LIF-B04`, `SYS-I05`, `COM-A04` | 1. Crisis signals/triage; 2. command/communication; 3. resource/mutual aid; 4. psychological recovery; 5. after-action improvement | Act under uncertainty; coordinate community support; learn after crisis | Individual toughness cannot replace systems, support, and recovery time | ☐ Lead crisis exercise; ☐ implement after-action improvements |
| `LIF-A03` Meaning, vocation, leisure, beauty, service, mortality, coherent life | `LIF-B01`, `PHI-B04`, `REL-I06`, `ART-B01` | 1. Flourishing traditions; 2. vocation/work/service; 3. leisure/play/beauty; 4. suffering/mortality; 5. narrative/integrity | Articulate examined life commitments; balance roles; revise without incoherence | Meaning need not reduce to pleasure, productivity, status, or one universal formula | ☐ Write and defend life philosophy; ☐ align a year's choices |
| `LIF-E01` Long-horizon stewardship and intergenerational contribution | `LIF-A01`, `LIF-A02`, `LIF-A03` | 1. Decades/generations; 2. family/community institutions; 3. knowledge/wealth/heritage transfer; 4. succession/resilience; 5. legacy review | Build durable contribution; transfer capability responsibly; plan succession | Legacy is not only fame or inheritance; it includes institutions and people developed | ☐ Execute stewardship project; ☐ complete succession/handoff |
