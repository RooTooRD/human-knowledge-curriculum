# Geography Syllabus

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GEO-B01` Location, place, region, scale, distance, direction, pattern | `FND-B07`, `FND-B09` | 1. Absolute/relative location; 2. place/landscape; 3. formal/functional/perceptual region; 4. scale/distance; 5. distribution/pattern | Describe spatial relations; choose scale/region; recognize clustering/dispersion | Regions are analytical constructs, not always fixed natural units | ☐ Produce multiscale place analysis; ☐ explain pattern with evidence |
| `GEO-B02` Maps, projections, coordinates, symbols, scale, judgment | `GEO-B01`, `MAT-B03` | 1. Coordinates/orientation; 2. scale; 3. projection/distortion; 4. symbol/classification; 5. map rhetoric/source | Read/create maps; calculate scale; identify distortion/design effects | Every flat world map necessarily distorts something | ☐ Compare projections; ☐ create honest thematic map |
| `GEO-B03` Physical geography | `GEO-B01`, `ECS-B01` | 1. Landforms/tectonics; 2. climate/weather; 3. water; 4. soils/biomes; 5. coupled landscapes | Explain spatial physical patterns; read profiles/maps; connect processes | Environmental zones have gradients, not perfectly sharp borders | ☐ Analyze physical region; ☐ construct process map |
| `GEO-B04` Human geography | `GEO-B01`, `SOC-B01` | 1. Population; 2. settlement/urbanization; 3. migration; 4. culture/identity; 5. spatial networks/globalization | Analyze population/settlement patterns; explain migration; avoid spatial determinism | Maps of people do not explain patterns without history/institutions | ☐ Interpret demographic maps; ☐ compare migration explanations |
| `GEO-I01` Economic geography | `GEO-B04`, `ECO-B01` | 1. Resources/agriculture; 2. industrial location; 3. trade/value chains; 4. transport/logistics; 5. uneven development | Map production networks; explain location; assess regional inequality | Globalization does not make distance irrelevant | ☐ Trace product chain; ☐ analyze location decision |
| `GEO-I02` Political geography | `GEO-B04`, `POL-B01`, `HST-B05` | 1. Territory/border; 2. state/nation; 3. colonial spatial orders; 4. electoral/administrative space; 5. geopolitics | Analyze territorial claims; distinguish nation/state; evaluate geopolitical narratives | Borders are historically produced, not timeless natural facts | ☐ Build border-case dossier; ☐ critique deterministic geopolitics |
| `GEO-I03` Cultural, linguistic, religious, historical landscapes | `GEO-B04`, `LIN-I05`, `REL-B03` | 1. Diffusion/contact; 2. language landscapes; 3. sacred space; 4. memory/heritage; 5. place identity/contest | Read landscapes as layered evidence; map cultural change; compare meanings | Cultural areas are internally diverse and overlapping | ☐ Conduct landscape field reading; ☐ produce contextual cultural map |
| `GEO-I04` Environment-society, hazards, vulnerability, justice, sustainability | `GEO-B03`, `GEO-B04`, `SYS-I05` | 1. Human-environment models; 2. hazard/exposure; 3. vulnerability/adaptation; 4. environmental justice; 5. sustainability trade-offs | Diagnose coupled risk; map unequal burden; propose place-sensitive response | Disasters are not purely natural events | ☐ Complete vulnerability map; ☐ defend equitable intervention |
| `GEO-I05` GIS, remote sensing, spatial data, geostatistics | `GEO-B02`, `STA-I06`, `CSC-B04` | 1. Raster/vector/CRS; 2. collection/geocoding; 3. overlay/buffer/network; 4. remote imagery; 5. spatial statistics/error | Build GIS workflow; manage CRS; analyze spatial uncertainty | Map precision does not exceed source positional accuracy | ☐ Deliver reproducible GIS project; ☐ validate spatial output |
| `GEO-A01` Urban and regional systems, planning, housing, mobility, infrastructure | `GEO-I01`, `GEO-I04`, `ARC-I05` | 1. Urban form/land; 2. housing/segregation; 3. mobility/access; 4. infrastructure/services; 5. planning/governance | Analyze city systems; measure accessibility; compare planning interventions | Density alone does not guarantee affordability or sustainability | ☐ Produce neighborhood diagnosis; ☐ evaluate planning scenario |
| `GEO-A02` Spatial analysis, network geography, geocomputation | `GEO-I05`, `SYS-I02`, `STA-A02` | 1. Spatial dependence; 2. point/area models; 3. routing/accessibility; 4. simulation; 5. scalable geocomputation | Fit spatial models; analyze networks; avoid ecological/MAUP errors | Nearby observations violate ordinary independence assumptions | ☐ Complete advanced spatial analysis; ☐ test scale/boundary sensitivity |
| `GEO-A03` Fieldwork, participatory mapping, critical cartography | `GEO-I05`, `RSH-I06`, `PHI-I05` | 1. Field question/sampling; 2. observation/interview/GPS; 3. participatory mapping; 4. map power/silence; 5. ethics/return of results | Conduct ethical fieldwork; co-produce maps; expose cartographic choices | Participatory maps are not automatically representative or harmless | ☐ Deliver field/participatory atlas; ☐ document consent and exclusions |
| `GEO-E01` Integrative geographic research | `GEO-A01`, `GEO-A02`, `GEO-A03` | 1. Spatial question/theory; 2. multiscale data; 3. field/computational integration; 4. causal/interpretive synthesis; 5. public cartography | Produce original geographic explanation; integrate methods/scales; communicate spatial uncertainty | Adding a map does not make research geographic | ☐ Complete original thesis; ☐ pass method/community review |
