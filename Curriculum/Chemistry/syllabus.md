# Chemistry Syllabus

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CHM-B01` Matter, substances, mixtures, phases, measurement, safety | `PHY-B01`, `RSH-B04` | 1. Macroscopic/particle descriptions; 2. pure substances/mixtures; 3. phases/separation; 4. measurement/significant figures; 5. hazards, PPE, waste, emergency | Classify/separate matter; report measurements; execute safe risk assessment | “Chemical” does not mean artificial or dangerous | ☐ Complete separation lab; ☐ pass safety scenario/practical |
| `CHM-B02` Atoms, isotopes, ions, periodicity, amount | `CHM-B01`, `MAT-B03` | 1. Atomic evidence/models; 2. nuclei/isotopes; 3. electrons/ions; 4. periodic trends; 5. mole, molar mass, formula | Calculate particle/mole relations; predict trends/ions; interpret isotope data | Atoms are not miniature solar systems | ☐ Solve mole/isotope problems; ☐ explain periodic trends |
| `CHM-B03` Bonding, shape, forces, structure-property | `CHM-B02`, `PHY-B04` | 1. Ionic/covalent/metallic models; 2. Lewis/resonance/formal charge; 3. VSEPR/polarity; 4. intermolecular forces; 5. solids and properties | Draw/evaluate structures; predict geometry/polarity/properties | Bonds are not rigid sticks, and intermolecular forces are not bonds within molecules | ☐ Analyze unknown compounds; ☐ justify property predictions |
| `CHM-B04` Reactions, equations, stoichiometry, limiting reagents, yield | `CHM-B02`, `MAT-B04` | 1. Reaction evidence/types; 2. formulas/equations; 3. mole ratios; 4. limiting/excess; 5. theoretical/actual/percent yield | Balance/model reactions; calculate quantities; identify limiting reagent | Coefficients change amount, not compound identity | ☐ Complete stoichiometry set; ☐ run quantitative synthesis |
| `CHM-B05` Energy, entropy, equilibrium, acids, bases, solubility | `CHM-B04`, `PHY-B06` | 1. Enthalpy/calorimetry; 2. entropy/free energy; 3. equilibrium/Le Châtelier; 4. acid-base/pH/buffers; 5. solubility/complex equilibria | Calculate/shift equilibria; analyze energetics; prepare/evaluate buffers | Equilibrium is dynamic and does not mean equal concentrations | ☐ Solve coupled equilibria; ☐ validate by titration/calorimetry |
| `CHM-B06` Redox, electrochemistry, rates, and mechanisms | `CHM-B04`, `PHY-B04`, `MAT-B06` | 1. Oxidation states/balancing; 2. galvanic/electrolytic cells; 3. rate laws; 4. activation/catalysis; 5. elementary steps/mechanisms | Balance redox; calculate cell/rate quantities; infer mechanisms from data | Catalysts change rate/path, not equilibrium position | ☐ Construct/test cell; ☐ derive rate law/mechanism |
| `CHM-I01` Quantum chemistry, orbitals, spectroscopy, symmetry | `CHM-B03`, `PHY-I06`, `MAT-I06` | 1. Wave mechanics/orbitals; 2. many-electron atoms; 3. MO/valence bonding; 4. symmetry; 5. rotational/vibrational/electronic spectroscopy | Build orbital models; predict spectra/selection; connect quantum states to bonding | Orbitals are probability amplitudes, not electron trajectories | ☐ Solve atom/molecule models; ☐ assign a spectrum |
| `CHM-I02` Thermodynamics, statistical mechanics, kinetics, transport | `CHM-B05`, `CHM-B06`, `PHY-I04` | 1. Laws/potentials; 2. phase/chemical equilibria; 3. molecular partition functions; 4. reaction dynamics; 5. diffusion/transport | Derive equilibrium/kinetic relations; interpret molecular basis; calculate transport | Thermodynamic feasibility says nothing by itself about speed | ☐ Complete derivations/data analysis; ☐ connect micro/macro models |
| `CHM-I03` Organic structure, stereochemistry, reactivity, synthesis, biomolecules | `CHM-B03`, `CHM-B06` | 1. Functional groups/nomenclature; 2. conformation/stereochemistry; 3. acid-base/nucleophile/electrophile; 4. substitution/addition/elimination/carbonyl; 5. synthesis/spectra/biomolecules | Predict mechanisms/products; design short syntheses; assign structures | Curved arrows move electron pairs, not atoms | ☐ Solve mechanism/synthesis set; ☐ identify unknown spectroscopically |
| `CHM-A01` Advanced organic synthesis and chemical biology | `CHM-I03`, `CHM-I01` | 1. Retrosynthesis/selectivity; 2. pericyclic/radical reactions; 3. organometallic/catalytic methods; 4. protecting/group strategy; 5. probes and chemical biology | Design/select syntheses; justify selectivity; evaluate modern methods | More synthetic steps do not mean greater sophistication or feasibility | ☐ Defend full route; ☐ execute/critique literature synthesis |
| `CHM-I04` Inorganic, coordination, organometallic, solid-state | `CHM-I01`, `CHM-B06` | 1. Main-group chemistry; 2. coordination/ligand fields; 3. organometallic bonding; 4. catalytic cycles; 5. lattices/defects/materials | Predict structure/reactivity/magnetism; analyze catalytic cycles; relate solids to properties | Oxidation state is formal bookkeeping, not always actual charge | ☐ Analyze complexes/cycles; ☐ characterize inorganic material |
| `CHM-I05` Analytical chemistry | `CHM-B05`, `CHM-I01`, `STA-I03` | 1. Sampling/calibration; 2. titrimetric/electrochemical; 3. spectroscopy; 4. chromatography/mass spectrometry; 5. validation/detection/uncertainty | Choose/validate methods; quantify analytes; interpret instrument data | Instrument precision cannot overcome biased sampling | ☐ Validate analytical method; ☐ identify/quantify blind samples |
| `CHM-I06` Polymer, colloid, surface, nano, and materials chemistry | `CHM-I02`, `CHM-I04` | 1. Polymerization/architecture; 2. molecular weight/thermal behavior; 3. interfaces/adsorption; 4. colloids/self-assembly; 5. nanomaterials/characterization | Predict material behavior; select synthesis/characterization; relate scale to function | “Nano” alone does not imply a superior or hazardous property | ☐ Design material for specification; ☐ interpret characterization suite |
| `CHM-A02` Biochemistry | `CHM-I03`, `BIO-B01` | 1. Proteins/folding/enzymes; 2. nucleic acids; 3. membranes/transport; 4. metabolism/bioenergetics; 5. signaling/molecular recognition | Analyze enzyme/metabolic data; connect structure/function; trace energy/information | Metabolic pathways are regulated networks, not isolated memorized lists | ☐ Solve kinetics/metabolism problems; ☐ interpret molecular experiment |
| `CHM-A03` Environmental, atmospheric, geochemical, industrial, green chemistry | `CHM-I02`, `CHM-I05`, `ECS-I01` | 1. Air/water/soil reactions; 2. cycles/speciation/transport; 3. process chemistry; 4. pollution/fate/risk; 5. green metrics/lifecycle | Model chemical fate; evaluate processes; calculate green/impact metrics | Dilution moves pollutants but does not necessarily remove hazard | ☐ Complete fate/process balance; ☐ redesign chemistry by green principles |
| `CHM-A04` Computational chemistry and molecular modeling | `CHM-I01`, `MAT-A04`, `CSC-B03` | 1. Molecular mechanics; 2. electronic-structure methods; 3. basis/approximation; 4. dynamics/free energy; 5. verification and experiment comparison | Choose methods by scale/accuracy; run reproducible calculations; interpret uncertainty | More expensive computation is not automatically more accurate | ☐ Reproduce benchmark; ☐ defend method/convergence |
| `CHM-E01` Chemical research design, synthesis, characterization, literature | `CHM-A01`, `CHM-I05`, `RSH-E01` | 1. Novel question/search; 2. route/method and safety; 3. execution/optimization; 4. orthogonal characterization; 5. mechanistic claim/publication | Conduct original chemical inquiry; prove identity/purity; make calibrated mechanism claims | A desired spectrum alone does not establish identity and purity | ☐ Deliver reproducible research dossier; ☐ pass oral/lab defense |
