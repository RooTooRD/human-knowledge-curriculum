# Finance Syllabus

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `FIN-B01` Money, accounts, payments, interest, inflation, credit, institutions | `FND-I02`, `ECO-B03` | 1. Money/payment rails; 2. account/statement; 3. simple/compound interest; 4. inflation/real value; 5. credit scores/lenders/regulators | Reconcile accounts; calculate interest/real cost; compare credit | Minimum payment is not a repayment plan | ☐ Reconcile statement; ☐ compare loan APR/cash flow |
| `FIN-B02` Budgeting, cash flow, reserves, debt, records | `FIN-B01`, `MAT-B03` | 1. Income/expense/cash timing; 2. needs/goals; 3. budget systems; 4. emergency reserve/debt strategy; 5. records/review | Build realistic cash plan; prioritize debt/reserve; maintain records | Budget is a feedback plan, not punishment or exact prediction | ☐ Produce three-month budget; ☐ handle shock scenario |
| `FIN-B03` Insurance, pooling, fraud, consumer protection, safety | `FIN-B01`, `STA-B03`, `SEC-B02` | 1. Risk/pooling; 2. premium/deductible/limit/exclusion; 3. life/health/property/liability; 4. claims; 5. scams/complaints | Compare coverage; choose retained/transferred risk; detect fraud | Insurance is for consequential uncertainty, not every small expense | ☐ Audit policy; ☐ respond to scam/claim |
| `FIN-B04` Saving, compounding, pensions, investing, diversification, fees, taxes | `FIN-B01`, `FIN-B02`, `STA-I01` | 1. Time horizon; 2. compound return; 3. asset classes; 4. diversification/indexing/fees; 5. pension/tax accounts/rebalancing | Build diversified plan; calculate real after-fee return; align risk/horizon | Diversification reduces specific risk, not all loss | ☐ Construct investment policy; ☐ compare fee/tax scenarios |
| `FIN-B05` Housing, mortgages, education, major purchases, lifecycle planning | `FIN-B02`, `FIN-B04` | 1. Total ownership cost; 2. rent/buy; 3. mortgage/amortization; 4. education/major purchase financing; 5. estate/beneficiary lifecycle | Calculate full cost; compare financing; plan across life events | Affordable monthly payment does not imply affordable total cost | ☐ Analyze rent-buy/mortgage; ☐ create lifecycle plan |
| `FIN-I01` Time value, DCF, bonds, equities, projects | `FIN-B04`, `MAT-B06` | 1. Present/future value; 2. annuity/perpetuity; 3. bond yield/duration; 4. equity cash flows; 5. NPV/IRR | Value cash flows/securities/projects; calculate sensitivity; choose decision rule | IRR can misrank projects and assumes reinvestment structure | ☐ Complete valuation model; ☐ defend assumptions |
| `FIN-I02` Risk-return, portfolios, asset pricing, efficiency, behavior | `FIN-I01`, `STA-I04` | 1. Return/risk/covariance; 2. portfolio frontier; 3. factor pricing; 4. market efficiency; 5. behavioral anomalies | Construct portfolios; estimate factor exposure; distinguish evidence on efficiency | Past high returns do not imply skill or future return | ☐ Optimize/evaluate portfolio; ☐ analyze anomaly claim |
| `FIN-I03` Statements, corporate financing, structure, payout, governance | `FIN-I01`, `BUS-B02` | 1. Statement linkage; 2. working capital; 3. investment decisions; 4. debt/equity/payout; 5. agency/governance | Forecast cash flows; evaluate financing; diagnose governance incentives | Accounting earnings are not identical to cash or value creation | ☐ Build integrated model; ☐ recommend financing/governance |
| `FIN-I04` Derivatives, hedging, options, futures, risk management | `FIN-I02`, `STA-I05` | 1. Forwards/futures; 2. options/payoffs; 3. no-arbitrage/binomial; 4. Black-Scholes/Greeks; 5. hedging/counterparty/model risk | Price simple derivatives; design hedges; explain residual risk | Hedging changes risk exposure and may sacrifice upside; it does not erase risk | ☐ Price/replicate option; ☐ construct hedge under scenarios |
| `FIN-I05` Banking, monetary transmission, liquidity, leverage, stability | `FIN-I01`, `ECO-I05` | 1. Bank balance sheet; 2. maturity/liquidity transformation; 3. capital/leverage; 4. monetary transmission; 5. regulation/stress/crisis | Analyze bank risk; perform stress test; explain policy transmission | Capital and liquidity are distinct defenses | ☐ Stress-test bank; ☐ trace rate change through system |
| `FIN-A01` International, public, Islamic, development, sustainable finance | `FIN-I03`, `FIN-I05`, `ECO-I06` | 1. FX/cross-border risk; 2. sovereign/municipal finance; 3. Islamic contracts/principles; 4. development finance; 5. climate/impact instruments | Compare financing institutions; structure compliant instruments; evaluate impact additionality | Labels such as green or sharia-compliant require substantive criteria, not branding | ☐ Analyze instrument; ☐ build financing plan |
| `FIN-A02` Fintech, digital assets, algorithmic markets, regulation, cyber risk | `FIN-I05`, `CSC-I07`, `LAW-I06`, `SEC-I04` | 1. Digital payments/lending; 2. blockchain/tokens; 3. exchanges/market microstructure; 4. automated advice/trading; 5. regulation/custody/cyber | Evaluate fintech architecture/business risk; trace token claims; assess regulation/security | Blockchain does not remove governance, custody, oracle, or fraud risk | ☐ Audit fintech product; ☐ model failure/consumer protection |
| `FIN-A03` Financial history, crises, bubbles, inequality, political economy | `FIN-I05`, `HST-I08`, `ECO-A02` | 1. Money/debt history; 2. bubbles/manias; 3. banking/sovereign crises; 4. reform/innovation cycles; 5. wealth/power | Reconstruct crisis mechanisms; compare episodes; connect finance/distribution/politics | Similar price booms need not share identical causes | ☐ Write comparative crisis study; ☐ identify leading vulnerabilities |
| `FIN-E01` Advanced valuation, quantitative finance, empirical research | `FIN-I04`, `STA-A04`, `RSH-E01` | 1. Stochastic processes; 2. continuous-time pricing; 3. fixed-income/credit; 4. empirical asset pricing; 5. model validation/research | Derive/implement quantitative models; backtest without leakage; produce original finance research | Good backtests can arise from overfitting and survivorship bias | ☐ Deliver validated model/paper; ☐ pass independent backtest audit |
