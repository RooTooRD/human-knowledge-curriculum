# Psychology Syllabus

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `PSY-B01` Psychological science, levels, history, methods, ethics | `RSH-B03`, `BIO-B05` | 1. Behavior/experience; 2. biological/cognitive/social/cultural levels; 3. schools/history; 4. experiment/observation/measurement; 5. ethics/replication | Frame multi-level explanations; evaluate psychological evidence; identify ethical constraints | Introspection and common sense are evidence sources, not infallible explanations | ☐ Critique a study; ☐ build multi-level account |
| `PSY-B02` Sensation, perception, attention, consciousness, learning, memory | `PSY-B01`, `BIO-I07` | 1. Psychophysics/senses; 2. perceptual organization; 3. attention/consciousness; 4. conditioning/learning; 5. memory systems/forgetting | Interpret experiments; distinguish sensation/perception; design learning/memory test | Perception and memory reconstruct rather than copy reality | ☐ Analyze illusion/memory result; ☐ run ethical mini-experiment |
| `PSY-B03` Motivation, emotion, stress, self-regulation | `PSY-B01`, `BIO-B05` | 1. Drives/goals/incentives; 2. emotion components/theories; 3. stress/appraisal; 4. regulation/coping; 5. habits/willpower/context | Compare motivational/emotion theories; analyze stress response; design regulation strategy | Emotions are neither purely irrational nor universally transparent | ☐ Analyze case across mechanisms; ☐ evaluate self-regulation intervention |
| `PSY-I01` Lifespan development | `PSY-B02`, `BIO-I04` | 1. Prenatal/infancy; 2. cognition/language; 3. attachment/socialization; 4. adolescence/adulthood; 5. aging/context | Explain continuity/change; compare designs; evaluate nature/culture interaction | Developmental milestones are distributions, not exact universal deadlines | ☐ Interpret longitudinal evidence; ☐ produce lifespan case formulation |
| `PSY-I02` Intelligence, personality, differences, assessment, behavior genetics | `PSY-B02`, `BIO-I01`, `STA-I03` | 1. Trait/state; 2. intelligence models; 3. personality models; 4. reliability/validity/norms; 5. heritability/gene-environment | Interpret tests responsibly; compare trait models; explain heritability | Heritability within a population does not mean immutability or individual causation | ☐ Critique assessment; ☐ interpret behavior-genetic claim |
| `PSY-I03` Judgment, decisions, reasoning, heuristics, behavior change | `PSY-B02`, `STA-B03`, `LOG-I04` | 1. Normative/descriptive choice; 2. heuristics/biases; 3. risk/time; 4. expertise/group choice; 5. nudges and behavior-change ethics | Diagnose decision errors; design/test intervention; preserve agency | Bias labels can become hindsight stories without process evidence | ☐ Analyze real decision; ☐ preregister behavioral intervention |
| `PSY-I04` Social psychology | `PSY-B03`, `STA-B02` | 1. Self/attribution; 2. attitudes/persuasion; 3. conformity/obedience; 4. groups/prejudice; 5. attraction/relationships/conflict | Explain social behavior contextually; evaluate classic/replication evidence; reduce prejudice/conflict | Social effects are probabilistic and context-sensitive, not laws affecting everyone equally | ☐ Replication-focused literature review; ☐ design social intervention |
| `PSY-I05` Psychopathology, diagnosis, therapy, prevention, recovery | `PSY-B03`, `HLT-B01` | 1. Distress/disorder models; 2. classification/assessment; 3. mood/anxiety/psychosis; 4. personality/neurodevelopment/substance; 5. therapies/prevention/recovery | Discuss disorders accurately; compare treatments; recognize referral/crisis boundaries | Diagnosis is not identity and does not reveal one simple cause | ☐ Evidence-based case analysis; ☐ produce stigma-free referral guide |
| `PSY-I06` Biological psychology | `PSY-B02`, `BIO-I07` | 1. Genes/development; 2. neural systems; 3. hormones/homeostasis; 4. drugs/reward; 5. lesion/imaging/inference | Connect biology/behavior cautiously; interpret neuroscience methods; analyze drug effects | Brain activation does not prove necessity, specificity, or causation | ☐ Critique neuroimaging claim; ☐ map mechanism across levels |
| `PSY-I07` Cultural, community, educational, organizational, health, forensic psychology | `PSY-I01`, `PSY-I04`, `SOC-B01` | 1. Culture/community; 2. learning/school; 3. work/organization; 4. health behavior; 5. legal/forensic applications | Apply psychology contextually; adapt measures/interventions; identify professional limits | Findings from narrow samples do not automatically generalize globally | ☐ Compare application domains; ☐ culturally adapt intervention |
| `PSY-A01` Psychometrics, experiments, replication, meta-analysis, open science | `PSY-I02`, `STA-I04`, `RSH-A03` | 1. Scale construction; 2. factor/IRT orientation; 3. design/power; 4. replication/meta-analysis; 5. preregistration/open materials | Develop/validate measure; reproduce/meta-analyze evidence; diagnose research bias | Cronbach alpha alone does not establish one construct or validity | ☐ Validate a scale; ☐ reproduce and synthesize study line |
| `PSY-A02` Clinical evidence, mechanisms, ethics, lay limits | `PSY-I05`, `HLT-I05`, `PHI-A02` | 1. Trial/effectiveness evidence; 2. common/specific mechanisms; 3. formulation and culture; 4. ethics/risk; 5. professional competence and public claims | Appraise clinical claims; explain treatment mechanisms; maintain scope boundaries | Personal experience cannot establish comparative treatment efficacy | ☐ Appraise therapy evidence; ☐ resolve ethics/scope scenarios |
| `PSY-A03` Learning, memory, expertise, metacognition, education | `PSY-B02`, `LRN-A01`, `STA-A01` | 1. Memory mechanisms; 2. practice/feedback; 3. expertise/transfer; 4. metacognition/calibration; 5. classroom causal evidence | Translate learning evidence; design instruction; evaluate boundary conditions | “Learning styles” matching lacks support as a general prescription | ☐ Design evidence-grounded unit; ☐ test learning rather than preference |
| `PSY-A04` Advanced social, developmental, cultural, personality theory | `PSY-I01`, `PSY-I02`, `PSY-I04` | 1. Person-situation dynamics; 2. lifespan systems; 3. culture/self; 4. identity/power; 5. integrative longitudinal models | Compare advanced frameworks; construct multilevel theory; test moderation | Broad theories require risky predictions, not post-hoc fit to any outcome | ☐ Build/test integrative model; ☐ defend cross-cultural validity |
| `PSY-E01` Original psychological research | `PSY-A01`, `RSH-E01` | 1. Theory/question; 2. valid measure/design; 3. preregistration/ethics; 4. analysis/robustness; 5. open dissemination | Conduct original rigorous study; make calibrated psychological claims; share reproducibly | Novel result without reliable measurement is not cumulative knowledge | ☐ Complete thesis/artifacts; ☐ pass replication/defense |
