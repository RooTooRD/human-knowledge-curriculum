# Security Glossary

These are curriculum-scoped working definitions. They identify how a term is used
in its unit and the operation mastery requires; consult the unit’s advanced
reference for competing, historical, or specialist definitions.

| Unit | Terms |
|---|---|

| `SEC-B02` | **Personal digital hygiene:** Within Personal digital hygiene: authentication, updates, backup, phishing, privacy, the organizing beginner idea that connects password managers and MFA with updates and least privilege; in this curriculum it supports the ability to secure accounts/devices.<br>**authentication:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through privacy settings and account recovery; mastery includes the ability to recognize social attacks. |
