# Systems Science Glossary

These are curriculum-scoped working definitions. They identify how a term is used
in its unit and the operation mastery requires; consult the unit’s advanced
reference for competing, historical, or specialist definitions.

| Unit | Terms |
|---|---|
| `SYS-B01` | **Systems:** Within Systems, boundaries, stocks, flows, state, environment, and scale, the organizing beginner idea that connects whole/parts/relations with purpose and boundary; in this curriculum it supports the ability to define system models.<br>**boundaries:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through scale and hierarchy; mastery includes the ability to choose boundaries. |
| `SYS-B02` | **Causal loops:** Within Causal loops, feedback, delay, accumulation, and unintended consequences, the organizing beginner idea that connects causal links/polarity with reinforcing/balancing loops; in this curriculum it supports the ability to draw valid loop diagrams.<br>**feedback:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through policy resistance; mastery includes the ability to predict qualitative behavior. |
| `SYS-I01` | **System dynamics:** Within System dynamics, differential and difference models, and simulation, the organizing intermediate idea that connects stock-flow equations with discrete/continuous time; in this curriculum it supports the ability to translate diagrams to equations.<br>**differential:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through sensitivity and policy tests; mastery includes the ability to simulate/validate. |
| `SYS-I02` | **Networks:** Within Networks: structure, diffusion, centrality, contagion, and robustness, the organizing intermediate idea that connects nodes/edges/directed/weighted with degree/path/community/centrality; in this curriculum it supports the ability to calculate network measures.<br>**structure:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through robustness/intervention; mastery includes the ability to model diffusion. |
| `SYS-I03` | **Control:** Within Control, stability, observability, adaptation, and cybernetics, the organizing intermediate idea that connects plant/controller/reference with open/closed loop; in this curriculum it supports the ability to analyze feedback control.<br>**stability:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through adaptive control and requisite variety; mastery includes the ability to tune basic controller. |
| `SYS-I04` | **Emergence:** Within Emergence, self-organization, nonlinear dynamics, and complexity, the organizing intermediate idea that connects micro rules/macro patterns with nonlinearity and phase space; in this curriculum it supports the ability to demonstrate emergence.<br>**self-organization:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through complexity measures and limits; mastery includes the ability to analyze nonlinear regimes. |
| `SYS-I05` | **Risk:** Within Risk, uncertainty, resilience, redundancy, and failure propagation, the organizing intermediate idea that connects hazard/exposure/consequence with aleatory/epistemic uncertainty; in this curriculum it supports the ability to model failures.<br>**uncertainty:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through resilience and recovery; mastery includes the ability to quantify uncertainty. |
| `SYS-I06` | **Decision theory:** Within Decision theory, utility, value of information, and multi-criteria choice, the organizing intermediate idea that connects acts/states/outcomes with expected utility and risk attitude; in this curriculum it supports the ability to build decision models.<br>**utility:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through multi-criteria and robust choice; mastery includes the ability to calculate information value. |
| `SYS-A01` | **Game theory:** Within Game theory, strategic interaction, mechanism design, and collective action, the organizing advanced idea that connects strategic/normal games with nash and mixed strategies; in this curriculum it supports the ability to solve equilibria.<br>**strategic interaction:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through mechanisms, commons, cooperation; mastery includes the ability to predict strategic responses. |
| `SYS-A02` | **Operations research:** Within Operations research, scheduling, queues, inventory, and logistics, the organizing advanced idea that connects linear/integer programs with networks/routing; in this curriculum it supports the ability to formulate operational models.<br>**scheduling:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through scheduling/simulation; mastery includes the ability to solve and interpret. |
| `SYS-A03` | **Agent-based modeling:** Within Agent-based modeling, cellular automata, and evolutionary systems, the organizing advanced idea that connects agents/rules/environment with cellular automata; in this curriculum it supports the ability to implement ABMs.<br>**cellular automata:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through calibration/validation/experiments; mastery includes the ability to connect rules to patterns. |
| `SYS-A04` | **Scenario analysis:** Within Scenario analysis, forecasting, foresight, and deep uncertainty, the organizing advanced idea that connects forecast target/base rate with trend/model/judgment ensembles; in this curriculum it supports the ability to produce calibrated forecasts.<br>**forecasting:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through horizon scanning and update; mastery includes the ability to construct coherent scenarios. |
| `SYS-A05` | **Systems intervention:** Within Systems intervention, leverage points, governance, and evaluation, the organizing advanced idea that connects problem/system framing with leverage points and behavior; in this curriculum it supports the ability to design ethical interventions.<br>**leverage points:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through monitoring, adaptation, exit; mastery includes the ability to anticipate adaptation. |
| `SYS-E01` | **Complex adaptive systems research synthesis:** Within Complex adaptive systems research and transdisciplinary synthesis, the organizing expert idea that connects multi-level question with cross-domain concepts and commensurability; in this curriculum it supports the ability to integrate disciplines without erasing methods.<br>**transdisciplinary synthesis:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through reflexive synthesis; mastery includes the ability to produce actionable uncertain knowledge. |
