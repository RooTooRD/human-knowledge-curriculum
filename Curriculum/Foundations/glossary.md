# Foundations Glossary

These are curriculum-scoped working definitions. They identify how a term is used
in its unit and the operation mastery requires; consult the unit’s advanced
reference for competing, historical, or specialist definitions.

| Unit | Terms |
|---|---|
| `FND-B01` | **Language:** Within Language, reference, and ordinary meaning, the organizing beginner idea that connects signs and referents—objects, actions, properties, names with words in context—literal meaning, convention, deixis, polysemy; in this curriculum it supports the ability to identify what common words and sentences refer to.<br>**reference:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through utterances—speaker, listener, intent, shared context, misunderstanding; mastery includes the ability to restate an utterance while preserving its ordinary meaning. |
| `FND-B05` | **Reading words:** Within Reading words, sentences, paragraphs, and instructions, the organizing beginner idea that connects decoding and fluent phrasing with sentence roles, connectors, pronouns, and negation; in this curriculum it supports the ability to read ordinary prose accurately.<br>**sentences:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through procedural language, conditions, warnings, and verification; mastery includes the ability to identify a paragraph's main point and support. |
| `FND-B09` | **Reading tables:** Within Reading tables, diagrams, maps, and interfaces, the organizing beginner idea that connects rows, columns, headers, units, totals, and missing values with legends, axes, arrows, nodes, layers, and scale; in this curriculum it supports the ability to extract a precise value or relationship from a visual representation.<br>**diagrams:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through interface hierarchy, state, controls, feedback, and error messages; mastery includes the ability to translate between prose and a simple diagram. |
| `FND-I01` | **Information navigation orientation:** Within Information navigation and source orientation, the organizing intermediate idea that connects information need and search vocabulary with contents, index, abstract, headings, metadata, and hyperlinks; in this curriculum it supports the ability to locate a specific fact and an explanatory source efficiently.<br>**source orientation:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through author, publisher, date, genre, purpose, and citation trail; mastery includes the ability to revise a failed query. |
| `FND-B06` | **Vocabulary building use:** Within Vocabulary building and dictionary use, the organizing beginner idea that connects context clues and word families with denotation, connotation, register, and collocation; in this curriculum it supports the ability to infer a provisional meaning from context.<br>**dictionary use:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through personal vocabulary capture, retrieval, and use; mastery includes the ability to select the relevant dictionary sense. |
| `FND-B02` | **Counting:** Within Counting, quantity, comparison, and estimation, the organizing beginner idea that connects one-to-one counting and cardinality with more, less, equal, ordering, and zero; in this curriculum it supports the ability to count finite collections accurately.<br>**quantity:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through exact count versus estimate, bounds, and reasonableness; mastery includes the ability to compare quantities. |
| `FND-B07` | **Time:** Within Time, scale, measurement, and spatial orientation, the organizing beginner idea that connects before/after, duration, calendars, clocks, and timelines with length, mass, volume, temperature, and unit comparison; in this curriculum it supports the ability to read common measures and schedules.<br>**scale:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through human, microscopic, geographic, planetary, and historical scales; mastery includes the ability to convert within familiar unit systems. |
| `FND-I02` | **Everyday quantitative judgment:** Within Everyday quantitative judgment, the organizing intermediate idea that connects rates, ratios, percentages, and percentage points with averages, ranges, totals, and denominators; in this curriculum it supports the ability to compare quantities on a common basis.<br>**quantitative judgment:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through unit prices, capacity, time, risk, and sanity checks; mastery includes the ability to choose the relevant denominator. |
| `FND-B03` | **Observation:** Within Observation, description, classification, and pattern, the organizing beginner idea that connects observation versus inference with descriptive attributes and measurement; in this curriculum it supports the ability to produce descriptions that separate observation from interpretation.<br>**description:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through similarity, difference, sequence, repetition, and anomaly; mastery includes the ability to construct and revise a classification rule. |
| `FND-B10` | **Questions:** Within Questions, uncertainty, and checking, the organizing beginner idea that connects open, closed, clarifying, causal, evaluative, and procedural questions with known, unknown, uncertain, and unknowable; in this curriculum it supports the ability to turn confusion into a precise question.<br>**uncertainty:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through cross-checking, reverse operations, independent confirmation, and error logs; mastery includes the ability to express the source and degree of uncertainty. |
| `FND-I03` | **Fact:** Within Fact, model, interpretation, opinion, and value, the organizing intermediate idea that connects observable claims and records with models, assumptions, idealization, and scope; in this curriculum it supports the ability to classify components of a complex statement.<br>**model:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through preferences, evaluations, norms, and mixed claims; mastery includes the ability to state what evidence could bear on each component. |
| `FND-B04` | **Agency:** Within Agency, goals, habits, and responsibility, the organizing beginner idea that connects needs, wants, values, and goals with choice, constraint, consequence, and responsibility; in this curriculum it supports the ability to convert an aspiration into a specific controllable goal.<br>**goals:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through planning, action, reflection, and revision; mastery includes the ability to distinguish controllable action from external constraint. |
| `FND-B08` | **Basic digital-device operation:** Within Basic digital-device and file operation, the organizing beginner idea that connects hardware, operating system, application, account, and network with input, navigation, settings, accessibility, and updates; in this curriculum it supports the ability to operate a common device without losing work.<br>**file operation:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through local/cloud storage, synchronization, backup, and safe shutdown; mastery includes the ability to organize and retrieve files. |
| `FND-I04` | **Personal safety:** Within Personal safety, consent, boundaries, and help-seeking, the organizing intermediate idea that connects hazard, warning sign, immediate danger, and precaution with consent as informed, voluntary, specific, ongoing, and revocable; in this curriculum it supports the ability to recognize situations requiring exit or help.<br>**consent:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through trusted people, emergency services, documentation, reporting, and bystander action; mastery includes the ability to communicate and respect a boundary. |
| `FND-I05` | **Civic orientation:** Within Civic and institutional orientation, the organizing intermediate idea that connects household, school, workplace, association, market, court, municipality, and state with roles, rules, records, offices, jurisdictions, and escalation; in this curriculum it supports the ability to identify which institution has authority over an ordinary problem.<br>**institutional orientation:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through identification, contracts, licenses, voting, and public information; mastery includes the ability to navigate a basic administrative process. |
| `FND-I06` | **Integrated foundational literacy:** Within Integrated foundational literacy, the organizing intermediate idea that connects read a mixed prose/numeric/visual brief with identify claims, uncertainty, risk, and responsible institution; in this curriculum it supports the ability to complete an unfamiliar everyday knowledge task from question to checked action.<br>**foundational literacy:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through decide, communicate, act, and review; mastery includes the ability to integrate language, quantity, visuals, safety, and institutional reasoning. |
