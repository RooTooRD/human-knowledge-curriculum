# Law Syllabus

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `LAW-B01` Rules, norms, rights, duties, powers, liability, jurisdiction, institutions | `FND-I05`, `LOG-B01` | 1. Legal/normative rule; 2. right/duty/power/immunity; 3. liability/remedy; 4. jurisdiction/venue; 5. courts/agencies/profession | Classify legal relations; identify institution/remedy; distinguish legal and moral claim | Having suffered harm does not automatically establish a legal cause of action | ☐ Map everyday dispute; ☐ identify right, duty, forum, remedy |
| `LAW-B02` Sources of law | `LAW-B01`, `HST-B02` | 1. Constitution/statute; 2. regulation; 3. precedent; 4. custom/religious law; 5. treaty/hierarchy/conflict | Locate controlling authority; rank sources; update validity/history | A quoted rule may be obsolete, nonbinding, or outside jurisdiction | ☐ Trace rule to current authority; ☐ resolve source conflict |
| `LAW-B03` Persons, identity, capacity, privacy, property, agreements, remedies | `LAW-B01`, `FND-I04` | 1. Natural/legal persons; 2. capacity/guardianship; 3. privacy/data; 4. possession/property; 5. consent/agreement/remedy | Recognize legal status and basic interests; read ordinary agreement; preserve rights/records | Signature does not validate every illegal, unconscionable, or unconsented term | ☐ Analyze consumer scenario; ☐ draft issue/help checklist |
| `LAW-I01` Legal reading, issues, rules, analogy, interpretation, argument | `LAW-B02`, `LOG-B03`, `WRT-I01` | 1. Facts/procedure/holding; 2. issue/rule synthesis; 3. precedent/analogy; 4. textual/purposive interpretation; 5. IRAC and counterargument | Brief cases; synthesize rules; argue both sides from authority | A case's outcome is not the same as its binding holding | ☐ Brief/synthesize case line; ☐ write balanced legal analysis |
| `LAW-I02` Constitutional law | `LAW-B02`, `POL-B02` | 1. Founding/amendment; 2. separation/checks; 3. federal/unitary allocation; 4. judicial review; 5. rights/emergency | Analyze constitutional disputes; compare systems; test proportionality/tiers | Constitutional text does not interpret or enforce itself | ☐ Decide moot problem; ☐ compare two constitutional systems |
| `LAW-I03` Administrative and public law | `LAW-I02`, `POL-I05` | 1. Delegation/authority; 2. rulemaking; 3. adjudication/due process; 4. discretion/reasons; 5. judicial/legislative oversight | Verify agency authority; assess procedure/reasoning; design accountable regulation | Expertise does not place administration beyond legal review | ☐ Audit agency action; ☐ draft lawful process |
| `LAW-I04` Private law | `LAW-B03`, `ECO-B02` | 1. Contract formation/defense/remedy; 2. tort duty/breach/causation; 3. property/title/use; 4. family/inheritance; 5. commercial transactions | Analyze private claims; calculate remedies; compare allocation/incentives | Moral blame and legal liability do not perfectly coincide | ☐ Solve mixed private-law case; ☐ draft/review agreement |
| `LAW-I05` Criminal law and procedure | `LAW-B03`, `PHI-B04` | 1. Act/mental state/defenses; 2. offenses/inchoate/complicity; 3. investigation/search; 4. charge/trial/evidence; 5. punishment/rights | Analyze culpability; protect procedure; evaluate evidence/punishment | Suspicion, arrest, charge, conviction, and guilt are distinct | ☐ Argue prosecution/defense; ☐ audit procedure |
| `LAW-I06` Corporate, labor, competition, consumer, tax, technology, data law | `LAW-I03`, `LAW-I04`, `BUS-B01` | 1. Entities/governance; 2. employment/labor; 3. competition/consumer; 4. taxation; 5. IP/platform/data/cyber | Spot regulatory obligations; compare entity/transaction structures; assess technology compliance | Incorporation does not erase all individual accountability | ☐ Produce compliance map; ☐ analyze platform/business case |
| `LAW-I07` Civil procedure, evidence, advocacy, negotiation, mediation | `LAW-I01`, `COM-I07`, `STA-I03` | 1. Pleading/jurisdiction; 2. discovery/motions; 3. admissibility/proof; 4. trial/appeal; 5. settlement/ADR | Plan litigation; assess evidence; advocate/negotiate ethically | Relevant evidence may still be inadmissible, and admissible evidence may be weak | ☐ Conduct moot procedure/hearing; ☐ negotiate reasoned settlement |
| `LAW-A01` Comparative legal systems | `LAW-B02`, `HST-I09`, `ISL-I04` | 1. Civil/common traditions; 2. customary/Indigenous; 3. Islamic law; 4. mixed/transnational systems; 5. transplantation/pluralism | Compare sources/institutions; analyze legal pluralism; avoid ethnocentric ranking | Legal families are ideal types with internal variation | ☐ Comparative dossier; ☐ solve plural-jurisdiction problem |
| `LAW-A02` Jurisprudence | `LAW-I01`, `PHI-I06` | 1. Positivism/natural law; 2. realism; 3. interpretation; 4. rights/justice; 5. critical/feminist/postcolonial theories | Reconstruct theories; apply them to hard cases; distinguish validity/merit | Legal positivism does not mean every valid law is morally justified | ☐ Debate hard case across theories; ☐ defend jurisprudential position |
| `LAW-A03` International public, human-rights, humanitarian, criminal, force law | `LAW-I02`, `POL-I06` | 1. Sources/subjects; 2. treaties/responsibility; 3. human rights; 4. conflict/use of force; 5. international crimes/courts | Determine applicable regimes; analyze jurisdiction/responsibility; distinguish legality/legitimacy | International law is neither world-government law nor simply optional diplomacy | ☐ Write international opinion; ☐ argue jurisdiction/compliance |
| `LAW-A04` Environmental, climate, energy, health, global administrative law | `LAW-I03`, `ECS-I04`, `HLT-I06` | 1. Pollution/resource regulation; 2. impact/precaution; 3. climate/energy; 4. health/emergency; 5. transnational administration | Navigate multi-level regimes; assess authority/evidence; design enforceable instrument | Declaring an emergency does not erase proportionality and oversight | ☐ Analyze transboundary case; ☐ draft monitored legal instrument |
| `LAW-A05` Legislation, institutional design, access to justice, reform | `LAW-I03`, `LAW-A02`, `POL-I04` | 1. Problem/authority; 2. statutory drafting; 3. institutional incentives; 4. implementation/enforcement; 5. access/evaluation/revision | Draft clear legislation; design institutions; assess access and effects | Passing a law does not ensure capacity, compliance, or justice | ☐ Draft bill/explanatory note; ☐ conduct implementation/access review |
| `LAW-E01` Advanced legal research and comparative argument | `LAW-A01`, `LAW-A02`, `RSH-E01` | 1. Novel legal question; 2. multilingual/multi-jurisdiction sources; 3. doctrinal/comparative method; 4. normative/empirical context; 5. publication/defense | Produce original source-grounded legal scholarship; handle conflicting authority; propose bounded reform | Citation density does not substitute for rule synthesis and analysis | ☐ Complete publishable article; ☐ pass comparative oral defense |
