# Earth, Climate, and Energy Syllabus

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ECS-B01` Earth as coupled system | `SYS-B01`, `GEO-B01`, `CHM-B01` | 1. Geosphere/hydrosphere/atmosphere/biosphere; 2. reservoirs/fluxes; 3. cycles/feedback; 4. deep time; 5. human forcing | Draw Earth budgets; compare timescales; trace cross-sphere changes | Earth equilibrium is dynamic, not unchanging balance | ☐ Build coupled-system diagram; ☐ close a material budget |
| `ECS-B02` Minerals, rocks, tectonics, structures, surface processes | `ECS-B01`, `PHY-B03` | 1. Minerals/rock cycle; 2. Earth interior; 3. plate evidence/mechanisms; 4. deformation/earthquakes/volcanoes; 5. weathering/erosion/sediment | Identify materials/processes; reconstruct tectonic setting; interpret geologic evidence | Continents do not drift through static ocean crust | ☐ Read geologic map/section; ☐ explain a plate boundary |
| `ECS-B03` Atmosphere, weather, circulation, clouds, forecasting | `ECS-B01`, `PHY-B06` | 1. Composition/vertical structure; 2. pressure, humidity, stability; 3. radiation/convection; 4. global circulation/fronts/storms; 5. observations/models/forecasts | Interpret weather maps; explain systems; evaluate forecasts probabilistically | Weather and climate are different scales, not contradictory concepts | ☐ Produce forecast rationale; ☐ analyze storm development |
| `ECS-B04` Oceans, cryosphere, hydrologic cycle, water resources | `ECS-B01`, `PHY-B05` | 1. Water cycle/basin; 2. ocean properties/currents; 3. waves/tides/coasts; 4. glaciers/ice; 5. groundwater/use/quality | Trace water movement; analyze ocean/ice roles; construct water budget | Groundwater is not generally an underground river or inexhaustible reservoir | ☐ Close watershed budget; ☐ interpret ocean/cryosphere data |
| `ECS-I01` Biogeochemical cycles, soils, ecosystems, Earth history | `ECS-B02`, `ECS-B04`, `BIO-B05`, `CHM-B05` | 1. Carbon/nitrogen/phosphorus cycles; 2. soil formation/function; 3. ecosystem-Earth coupling; 4. stratigraphy/proxies; 5. mass extinctions/long history | Analyze cycles/proxies; connect soils/ecosystems; reconstruct environmental change | Geologic records are incomplete but not therefore uninterpretable | ☐ Analyze proxy sequence; ☐ model altered nutrient cycle |
| `ECS-I02` Radiation balance, greenhouse effect, feedback, climate dynamics | `ECS-B03`, `PHY-I04`, `SYS-B02` | 1. Solar/terrestrial radiation; 2. greenhouse physics; 3. albedo/water/carbon feedback; 4. circulation/ocean coupling; 5. variability/forcing/sensitivity | Calculate energy balance; explain greenhouse mechanism; classify forcing/feedback | Greenhouse warming is not caused by a physical “lid” trapping all heat | ☐ Derive simple climate model; ☐ explain observed forcing response |
| `ECS-I03` Paleoclimate, observations, models, attribution, projections | `ECS-I02`, `STA-A02`, `RSH-A02` | 1. Proxy records; 2. instrumental observations; 3. climate-model hierarchy; 4. detection/attribution; 5. ensembles/scenarios/uncertainty | Compare evidence streams; evaluate model skill; interpret projections conditionally | Model uncertainty does not imply ignorance or equal likelihood of all futures | ☐ Assess attribution evidence; ☐ communicate ensemble projection |
| `ECS-I04` Impacts, vulnerability, adaptation, mitigation, justice | `ECS-I03`, `GEO-I04`, `ECO-I03`, `PHI-I05` | 1. Hazard/exposure/vulnerability; 2. food/water/health/ecosystems; 3. adaptation limits; 4. mitigation portfolios; 5. responsibility/equity/just transition | Assess localized risk; compare responses; evaluate distributional consequences | Climate impact is not determined by hazard alone | ☐ Produce regional risk assessment; ☐ defend equitable response portfolio |
| `ECS-I05` Energy fundamentals, sources, conversion, efficiency, power, grids | `PHY-B04`, `PHY-B06`, `CHM-B06` | 1. Energy/work/power; 2. primary/final/useful energy; 3. conversion/efficiency; 4. demand/load; 5. electricity grids and balancing | Calculate energy/power/efficiency; read system flows; distinguish capacity and generation | Energy is conserved while useful energy quality degrades | ☐ Audit an energy service; ☐ balance a grid snapshot |
| `ECS-I06` Fossil, nuclear, renewable, storage, transmission, demand | `ECS-I05`, `ENG-I03` | 1. Coal/oil/gas chains; 2. fission/fuel cycle; 3. solar/wind/hydro/geothermal; 4. storage/transmission; 5. demand flexibility/reliability | Compare technologies across lifecycle/system metrics; design a balanced mix | Nameplate capacity cannot be compared directly with annual generation | ☐ Model an hourly energy mix; ☐ produce lifecycle comparison |
| `ECS-A01` Energy transition modeling, economics, policy, lifecycle | `ECS-I04`, `ECS-I06`, `ECO-I04`, `SYS-A04` | 1. Transition pathways; 2. cost/learning/finance; 3. carbon policy/standards; 4. lifecycle/material constraints; 5. scenarios/political feasibility | Build transition scenario; compare policy instruments; test assumptions | Lowest unit cost alone does not determine system-optimal transition | ☐ Deliver transparent pathway model; ☐ stress-test feasibility/equity |
| `ECS-I07` Natural hazards, disaster risk, resilience, early warning | `ECS-B02`, `ECS-B03`, `SYS-I05` | 1. Geologic/weather/hydrologic hazards; 2. exposure/vulnerability; 3. probability/cascades; 4. warning/evacuation; 5. recovery/build-back-better | Map risk; interpret warnings; design layered preparedness | Natural events become disasters through exposure and vulnerability | ☐ Complete multi-hazard plan; ☐ run warning decision exercise |
| `ECS-I08` Resources, pollution, biodiversity loss, land use, boundaries | `ECS-I01`, `CHM-A03`, `BIO-I05` | 1. Extraction/material flows; 2. air/water/soil pollution; 3. land/food systems; 4. biodiversity drivers; 5. planetary-boundary framework | Quantify material/pollution flows; evaluate trade-offs; identify thresholds | Recycling cannot eliminate growth, energy, contamination, and entropy constraints | ☐ Complete material-flow assessment; ☐ design prevention hierarchy |
| `ECS-A02` Geophysics, geochemistry, remote sensing, computation | `ECS-B02`, `CHM-A03`, `PHY-I08` | 1. Seismic/gravity/magnetic methods; 2. isotope/element systems; 3. satellite spectra/radar; 4. inverse problems; 5. Earth-system computation | Infer subsurface/environmental state; process remote data; quantify inverse uncertainty | Inverse solutions are often nonunique | ☐ Analyze geophysical/remote dataset; ☐ validate inference independently |
| `ECS-A03` Environmental governance and climate institutions | `ECS-I04`, `POL-I06`, `LAW-A04` | 1. Commons/property; 2. domestic agencies/law; 3. treaties/climate regime; 4. monitoring/finance/compliance; 5. participation/justice | Map governance authority; evaluate institutional effectiveness; propose implementable reform | Global agreement text is not the same as domestic implementation | ☐ Analyze one regime; ☐ design monitored governance reform |
| `ECS-E01` Earth-system and climate research frontiers | `ECS-I03`, `ECS-A02`, `RSH-E01` | 1. Frontier question; 2. observations/proxies/models; 3. coupled analysis; 4. uncertainty/attribution; 5. open data/public significance | Conduct original Earth-system inquiry; integrate model/observation; report uncertainty responsibly | Urgency does not justify overstating certainty | ☐ Produce reproducible study; ☐ defend policy relevance and limits |
