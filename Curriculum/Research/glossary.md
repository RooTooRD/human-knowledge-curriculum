# Research Glossary

These are curriculum-scoped working definitions. They identify how a term is used
in its unit and the operation mastery requires; consult the unit’s advanced
reference for competing, historical, or specialist definitions.

| Unit | Terms |
|---|---|
| `RSH-B01` | **Inquiry:** Within Inquiry, answerable questions, constructs, and scope, the organizing beginner idea that connects curiosity to problem with descriptive, causal, interpretive, evaluative, design questions; in this curriculum it supports the ability to formulate bounded answerable questions.<br>**answerable questions:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through scope, feasibility, stakes; mastery includes the ability to identify constructs and unit of analysis. |
| `RSH-B02` | **Search:** Within Search, discovery, libraries, databases, references, and citation trails, the organizing beginner idea that connects search concepts and synonyms with catalogs, scholarly databases, repositories; in this curriculum it supports the ability to execute reproducible searches.<br>**discovery:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through stopping rules; mastery includes the ability to follow citation networks. |
| `RSH-B03` | **Observation:** Within Observation, hypothesis, model, prediction, test, and explanation, the organizing beginner idea that connects observation and anomaly with hypothesis and alternative; in this curriculum it supports the ability to build a testable model.<br>**hypothesis:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through explanation and revision; mastery includes the ability to derive discriminating predictions. |
| `RSH-B04` | **Research ethics:** Within Research ethics, consent, integrity, authorship, and conflicts of interest, the organizing beginner idea that connects harm, benefit, justice, respect with informed consent and vulnerable participants; in this curriculum it supports the ability to identify ethical issues.<br>**consent:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through review and reporting; mastery includes the ability to construct consent and data protections. |
| `RSH-I01` | **Primary:** Within Primary, secondary, and tertiary sources; authority and provenance, the organizing intermediate idea that connects primary/secondary/tertiary by question with genre, venue, peer review; in this curriculum it supports the ability to select sources by evidential role.<br>**secondary:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through triangulation; mastery includes the ability to verify provenance. |
| `RSH-I02` | **Literature review:** Within Literature review, synthesis, disagreement, and gap identification, the organizing intermediate idea that connects review question and boundaries with coding concepts/methods/results; in this curriculum it supports the ability to build a synthesis matrix.<br>**synthesis:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through genuine gaps and contribution; mastery includes the ability to explain consensus/dispute. |
| `RSH-I03` | **Operationalization:** Within Operationalization, validity, reliability, sampling, and measurement, the organizing intermediate idea that connects construct to indicator with content/criterion/construct validity; in this curriculum it supports the ability to design and critique measures.<br>**validity:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through measurement invariance and reactivity; mastery includes the ability to estimate reliability. |
| `RSH-I04` | **Experimental design:** Within Experimental and quasi-experimental design, the organizing intermediate idea that connects treatment, control, assignment with blinding, placebo, compliance; in this curriculum it supports the ability to design controlled studies.<br>**quasi-experimental design:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through validity, power, and analysis plan; mastery includes the ability to identify validity threats. |
| `RSH-I05` | **Historical:** Within Historical, archival, textual, and comparative methods, the organizing intermediate idea that connects archive construction and source survival with internal/external criticism; in this curriculum it supports the ability to build source-based interpretations.<br>**archival:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through narrative and uncertainty; mastery includes the ability to corroborate and contextualize. |
