# Education Syllabus

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `EDU-B01` Purposes, learner variation, development, relationships | `LRN-B01`, `PSY-I01` | 1. Personal/civic/economic purposes; 2. development; 3. prior knowledge/variation; 4. teacher-learner relationship; 5. rights/expectations | Articulate educational aims; profile learners without deficit labels; build respectful relationship | High expectations and responsive support are compatible | ☐ Learner/aim analysis; ☐ defend inclusive teaching stance |
| `EDU-B02` Objectives, explanation, examples, questioning, practice, feedback | `EDU-B01`, `LRN-B06` | 1. Observable objectives; 2. explanation/modeling; 3. example/nonexample; 4. question/check; 5. guided/independent practice/feedback | Write aligned objectives; explain and model; use checks to adapt teaching | Asking “understand?” does not assess understanding | ☐ Teach micro-lesson; ☐ demonstrate evidence-based adjustment |
| `EDU-B03` Environment, motivation, belonging, expectations, culture | `EDU-B01`, `PSY-B03` | 1. Safety/belonging; 2. routines/norms; 3. motivation/agency; 4. expectation/feedback; 5. behavior/restorative response | Build predictable inclusive environment; support motivation; respond proportionately | Rewards and punishment alone do not build durable self-regulation | ☐ Design environment plan; ☐ handle classroom scenarios |
| `EDU-I01` Curriculum design | `EDU-B02`, `SYS-B01` | 1. End goals; 2. knowledge graph/prerequisites; 3. scope/sequence; 4. core/extension/coherence; 5. review/hidden curriculum | Build dependency-driven curriculum; justify inclusion/order; detect gaps/redundancy | A list of topics is not a coherent curriculum | ☐ Produce validated unit map; ☐ defend scope and sequence |
| `EDU-I02` Assessment | `EDU-B02`, `STA-I03` | 1. Construct/evidence; 2. formative/summative; 3. item/performance design; 4. rubric/grading; 5. validity/reliability/fairness | Design aligned assessments; score reliably; use results formatively | Objective-looking scores can measure the wrong construct precisely | ☐ Build/validate assessment; ☐ moderate scoring |
| `EDU-I03` Pedagogies | `EDU-B02`, `RSH-B03` | 1. Explicit instruction; 2. inquiry/problem learning; 3. discussion; 4. apprenticeship/project; 5. collaboration/blending | Select pedagogy by goal/novice level; implement with scaffolds; compare evidence | No pedagogy is universally best independent of content and learner expertise | ☐ Teach same goal two ways; ☐ justify and evaluate choice |
| `EDU-I04` Literacy, numeracy, disciplinary knowledge, misconceptions, change | `EDU-I03`, `LRN-I04` | 1. Reading/writing development; 2. numeracy; 3. disciplinary ways of knowing; 4. misconception diagnosis; 5. conceptual change/transfer | Teach foundational literacies in disciplines; elicit prior models; design conceptual change | Simply stating the correct answer rarely replaces a coherent misconception | ☐ Diagnose learner thinking; ☐ demonstrate conceptual-change lesson |
| `EDU-I05` Inclusion, disability, multilingualism, culture, differentiation, universal design | `EDU-B03`, `ARC-I01`, `LIN-I05` | 1. Barrier/social model; 2. UDL/accessibility; 3. multilingual pedagogy; 4. cultural responsiveness; 5. differentiation/accommodation | Remove barriers; plan multiple access/expression routes; distinguish accommodation/modification | Inclusion is not lowering intellectual expectations | ☐ Accessibility audit/redesign; ☐ teach inclusive lesson |
| `EDU-I06` Educational technology, media, AI, privacy, access, analytics | `EDU-I01`, `AIX-B02`, `SEC-I02` | 1. Learning need/technology fit; 2. multimedia/cognitive load; 3. platforms/access; 4. AI use/assessment; 5. privacy/analytics/governance | Evaluate edtech by learning evidence; integrate AI transparently; protect learner data | Digital novelty and engagement do not establish learning | ☐ Pilot/evaluate tool; ☐ complete privacy/equity review |
| `EDU-I07` Education systems | `EDU-B01`, `SOC-B03` | 1. Family/early learning; 2. school systems; 3. university/research; 4. vocational/workplace; 5. informal/lifelong/international comparison | Compare system purposes/governance; trace learner pathways; analyze institutional incentives | Schooling and education are not identical | ☐ Comparative system map; ☐ diagnose pathway failure |
| `EDU-A01` Teacher knowledge, lesson study, observation, mentoring, professional learning | `EDU-I03`, `RSH-I06` | 1. Content/pedagogical content knowledge; 2. planning/enactment; 3. observation/evidence; 4. lesson study/coaching; 5. professional inquiry | Analyze teaching without personality judgment; improve collaboratively; mentor with evidence | Years of experience do not automatically produce expertise without feedback | ☐ Complete lesson-study cycle; ☐ mentor/reflect from observation |
| `EDU-A02` Program evaluation, experiments, qualitative inquiry, improvement science | `EDU-I02`, `RSH-A01`, `STA-A01` | 1. Theory of change; 2. implementation/process; 3. causal impact; 4. qualitative/mixed evaluation; 5. iterative improvement/equity | Evaluate whether/how/for whom programs work; distinguish improvement/research; adapt responsibly | Rising scores without comparison or implementation evidence do not prove impact | ☐ Complete program evaluation; ☐ run improvement cycle |
| `EDU-A03` Education history, philosophy, policy, finance, governance, inequality | `EDU-I07`, `HST-I07`, `PHI-I06`, `POL-I04` | 1. Historical institutions; 2. educational philosophies; 3. policy/governance; 4. finance/markets; 5. access/segregation/inequality | Contextualize reforms; analyze values/incentives; evaluate distribution | Education alone cannot compensate for every social and economic inequality | ☐ Policy history/analysis; ☐ defend reform with equity/feasibility |
| `EDU-A04` Independent course, workshop, or public-learning design | `EDU-I01`, `EDU-I02`, `EDU-I05` | 1. Learner/context; 2. outcomes/graph; 3. lessons/materials; 4. assessment/access; 5. pilot/revision | Design, deliver, and revise complete learning experience; document alignment | Content expertise alone does not guarantee teachable sequence | ☐ Deliver full course/workshop; ☐ show learner evidence/revision |
| `EDU-E01` Educational research and institutional transformation | `EDU-A02`, `EDU-A03`, `RSH-E01` | 1. Systemic problem; 2. political/learning theory; 3. co-designed intervention; 4. implementation/causal/equity evidence; 5. scale/institutional learning | Produce original educational research; lead ethical transformation; distinguish scale from fidelity | Scaling a program is not copying it unchanged into new contexts | ☐ Complete transformation study; ☐ pass learner/educator/policy defense |
