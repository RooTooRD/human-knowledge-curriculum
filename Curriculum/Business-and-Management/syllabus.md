# Business and Management Syllabus

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BUS-B01` Organizations, stakeholders, value propositions, models, sectors | `FND-I05`, `ECO-B01` | 1. Organization/sector; 2. stakeholder and need; 3. value proposition; 4. revenue/cost/resource model; 5. mission and externality | Map organization/business model; identify value/cost/externality; compare sectors | Revenue, profit, value, and social benefit are distinct | ☐ Analyze two organizations; ☐ design coherent model |
| `BUS-B02` Accounting | `BUS-B01`, `FIN-B01` | 1. Equation/double entry; 2. income/balance/cash statements; 3. accrual/recognition; 4. cost/managerial accounting; 5. control/audit/interpretation | Record transactions; link statements; calculate costs/ratios; detect red flags | Profit does not equal cash flow | ☐ Complete accounting cycle; ☐ analyze statements |
| `BUS-B03` Customers, markets, segmentation, positioning, channels, ethics | `BUS-B01`, `PSY-B02` | 1. Customer problem/research; 2. segmentation/targeting; 3. positioning/brand; 4. product/price/channel/promotion; 5. ethics/privacy/measurement | Develop evidence-based market strategy; test positioning; evaluate ethical effects | Marketing is not merely promotion or manipulation | ☐ Produce research-backed plan; ☐ run/interpret market test |
| `BUS-B04` Operations, processes, capacity, quality, inventory, supply chains | `BUS-B01`, `SYS-B01` | 1. Process mapping; 2. capacity/bottleneck/flow; 3. variability/quality; 4. inventory/forecast; 5. sourcing/logistics/resilience | Measure/improve process; calculate capacity/inventory; map supply risk | Maximizing utilization everywhere can worsen flow and delay | ☐ Improve measured process; ☐ stress-test supply chain |
| `BUS-I01` Organizational behavior, culture, incentives, teams, power, change | `BUS-B01`, `SOC-I03`, `PSY-I04` | 1. Motivation/incentive; 2. teams/coordination; 3. culture/norms; 4. power/politics; 5. change/resistance | Diagnose behavior systemically; design teams/incentives; plan change | Resistance may carry valid information rather than mere stubbornness | ☐ Analyze organization case; ☐ propose monitored intervention |
| `BUS-I02` Management | `BUS-I01`, `SYS-I06` | 1. Goals/priorities; 2. organization/delegation; 3. staffing/development; 4. direction/decision; 5. control/learning | Set aligned goals; delegate with authority; make/review decisions | Management is not synonymous with supervision or control | ☐ Run planning cycle; ☐ demonstrate delegation/review |
| `BUS-I03` Strategy | `BUS-B01`, `ECO-I01` | 1. Industry/value chain; 2. resources/capabilities; 3. positioning/trade-offs; 4. corporate/platform strategy; 5. execution/adaptation | Diagnose competitive context; choose coherent trade-offs; link strategy to action | Strategy is not a list of aspirations or operational improvements | ☐ Write strategy diagnosis-choice-actions; ☐ test against competitor response |
| `BUS-I04` Entrepreneurship | `BUS-B03`, `RSH-I04`, `FIN-I01` | 1. Problem discovery; 2. assumption mapping/experiments; 3. MVP/product-market fit; 4. unit economics/funding; 5. scaling/failure/ethics | Test demand cheaply; calculate unit economics; choose funding/scale path | A product and enthusiastic feedback do not establish a viable business | ☐ Run customer experiments; ☐ defend evidence-based venture decision |
| `BUS-I05` Product management, design, technology, innovation, lifecycle | `BUS-I04`, `ARC-I02`, `CSC-I10` | 1. Product vision/outcome; 2. discovery/research; 3. prioritization/roadmap; 4. delivery/metrics; 5. lifecycle/retirement | Align user/business/technical evidence; prioritize transparently; manage lifecycle | A roadmap is not a guaranteed feature calendar | ☐ Deliver validated product brief; ☐ make metric-based iteration |
| `BUS-I06` Project management | `BUS-B04`, `COM-I07`, `SYS-A02` | 1. Charter/scope; 2. work/schedule/resources; 3. cost/procurement; 4. risk/quality/change; 5. agile/plan-driven/closure | Plan/track delivery; select method; manage change and handoff | Agile does not eliminate scope, estimates, risk, or accountability | ☐ Produce executable plan; ☐ recover simulated troubled project |
| `BUS-I07` Human resources and labor relations | `BUS-I01`, `LAW-I04` | 1. Workforce planning/job design; 2. fair hiring; 3. onboarding/development; 4. performance/pay; 5. labor relations/separation | Design valid fair people systems; conduct feedback; comply with rights | Performance ratings are measurements vulnerable to bias, not pure facts | ☐ Audit hiring/performance process; ☐ conduct difficult simulation |
| `BUS-I08` Business law, governance, compliance, ethics, sustainability, impact | `BUS-B02`, `LAW-I06`, `PHI-I05` | 1. Entity/fiduciary governance; 2. contract/regulatory duties; 3. compliance/control; 4. ethical stakeholder analysis; 5. sustainability/impact reporting | Build governance/compliance map; resolve ethical conflicts; verify impact claims | Legal compliance is a floor, not proof of ethical or sustainable conduct | ☐ Produce board risk brief; ☐ audit impact claim |
| `BUS-A01` International business, institutions, culture, geopolitics, supply chains | `BUS-I03`, `GEO-I01`, `POL-I06` | 1. Entry/ownership; 2. institutional/currency risk; 3. culture/management; 4. global tax/trade; 5. geopolitical/supply resilience | Select international strategy; assess country/institution risk; localize responsibly | National culture averages do not predict every person or firm | ☐ Write market-entry dossier; ☐ stress-test geopolitical shocks |
| `BUS-A02` Leadership, decision systems, crisis, resilience | `BUS-I02`, `COM-A03`, `SYS-I05` | 1. Authority/legitimacy/service; 2. vision/sensemaking; 3. decision rights/dissent; 4. crisis command/communication; 5. resilience/succession | Lead under uncertainty; create dissent and accountability; manage crisis/recovery | Charisma does not substitute for institutions, competence, or ethics | ☐ Lead crisis simulation; ☐ design succession/resilience system |
| `BUS-A03` Analytics, experiments, forecasting, evidence-based management | `BUS-B02`, `STA-A01`, `SYS-A04` | 1. Decision/metric; 2. data quality/dashboard; 3. experiment/causality; 4. forecast/scenario; 5. adoption/governance | Build decision-relevant analysis; run experiments; prevent metric gaming | What is easy to count may not be what matters | ☐ Deliver decision analysis; ☐ detect and repair perverse metric |
| `BUS-E01` Enterprise creation or transformation capstone | `BUS-I04`, `BUS-I06`, `BUS-A02`, `BUS-A03` | 1. Mandate/evidence; 2. strategy/model; 3. operating/financial/people design; 4. execution/change; 5. measured outcomes/governance | Create/transform an organization responsibly; integrate functions; adapt from evidence | A polished plan without implementation evidence is not transformation | ☐ Deliver operating enterprise/change; ☐ defend outcomes and externalities |
