# Islamic Studies Syllabus

| Unit | Prerequisites | Ordered lessons | Observable outcomes | Common misconception | Mastery checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ISL-B01` Arabia, Late Antiquity, Prophetic biography, revelation, first community | `HST-B05`, `REL-B02` | 1. Late-antique Arabia; 2. Meccan life/revelation; 3. Hijra/Medina; 4. treaties/conflicts/community; 5. death/succession/source questions | Construct chronology; contextualize sira; distinguish source strata and devotional/historical questions | Pre-Islamic Arabia was not culturally empty or isolated | ☐ Source-based timeline; ☐ analyze one sira episode |
| `ISL-B02` Qur'an | `ISL-B01`, `REL-B01` | 1. Revelation/recitation; 2. sura structure/Meccan-Medinan; 3. themes/narratives; 4. collection/qira'at; 5. translation/reception | Navigate Qur'an; identify themes/form; explain collection/translation issues | The Qur'an is not arranged as a chronological biography | ☐ Thematic close reading; ☐ explain translation choices |
| `ISL-B03` Sunnah and hadith | `ISL-B01`, `HST-B02` | 1. Sunnah/hadith roles; 2. isnad/matn; 3. transmission/collection; 4. major collections/types; 5. basic authenticity/use debates | Distinguish hadith/sunnah; read report structure; describe transmission cautiously | Presence in a collection does not end every question of meaning/application | ☐ Annotate reports; ☐ trace basic transmission history |
| `ISL-B04` Worship, ethics, spirituality, community, diversity | `ISL-B02`, `ISL-B03` | 1. Faith/practice; 2. prayer/fast/alms/pilgrimage; 3. character/intention; 4. family/community; 5. Sunni/Shi'i/Ibadi and cultural diversity | Explain core practice and purpose; distinguish shared core/variation; avoid cultural essentialism | Muslim practice is neither culturally uniform nor reducible to legal rules | ☐ Compare practices responsibly; ☐ connect source, ethics, ritual |
| `ISL-I01` Arabic for Islamic sources | `LIN-B02`, `ISL-B02` | 1. Script/phonology; 2. morphology/roots; 3. nominal/verbal syntax; 4. Qur'anic/classical vocabulary; 5. dictionary/reading strategies | Parse basic clauses; use lexica; compare translations against Arabic | Root etymology alone does not determine contextual meaning | ☐ Parse selected text; ☐ justify translation |
| `ISL-I02` Qur'anic sciences, exegesis, hermeneutics | `ISL-B02`, `RSH-I05` | 1. Revelation occasions/order; 2. qira'at/text sciences; 3. language/rhetoric; 4. tafsir traditions; 5. legal/theological/modern hermeneutics | Evaluate tafsir methods; contextualize interpretations; compare evidence | Citing a verse without linguistic/textual context is not exegesis | ☐ Comparative tafsir study; ☐ produce method-explicit exegesis |
| `ISL-I03` Hadith sciences | `ISL-B03`, `LOG-I04` | 1. Transmission categories; 2. narrator criticism; 3. continuity/corroboration; 4. matn/hidden defects; 5. authority/modern scholarship | Analyze isnad/matn; explain grading; distinguish authenticity and application | Hadith criticism was not only checking narrator morality or chain presence | ☐ Complete takhrij-style dossier; ☐ defend calibrated grading |
| `ISL-I04` Jurisprudence | `ISL-B04`, `LAW-B01` | 1. Fiqh/sharia/fatwa; 2. Qur'an/Sunnah/consensus/analogy; 3. madhhabs/disagreement; 4. usul/maxims/objectives; 5. worship/transactions/application | Distinguish legal categories/authority; reconstruct rulings; explain legitimate disagreement | Sharia is not identical to one code, state law, or jurist opinion | ☐ Compare school reasoning; ☐ solve bounded legal problem |
| `ISL-I05` Theology and philosophy | `ISL-B04`, `PHI-A04` | 1. Early debates; 2. Mu'tazili/Ash'ari/Maturidi/Athari; 3. falsafa/logic; 4. later synthesis/critique; 5. modern questions | Reconstruct arguments; compare schools without caricature; trace exchanges | Labels conceal internal development and shared premises | ☐ Primary-text argument map; ☐ comparative oral examination |
| `ISL-I06` Spirituality and Sufism | `ISL-B04`, `REL-I07` | 1. Qur'anic virtue/ihsan; 2. asceticism; 3. stations/states/remembrance; 4. orders/masters/texts; 5. reform/critique/diversity | Explain doctrines/practices historically; compare traditions; distinguish normative and sociological claims | Sufism is neither one institution nor simply opposed to law | ☐ Analyze source/practice; ☐ compare critique and defense |
| `ISL-I07` Islamic civilization, c. 600–1500 | `HST-I01`, `ISL-B01` | 1. Caliphates/polities; 2. cities/economies/society; 3. law/education; 4. science/philosophy/literature; 5. art/global networks | Explain institutional/cultural diversity; trace knowledge/trade; avoid golden-age simplification | Islamic civilization was not a single ethnic empire or static golden age | ☐ Regional comparative atlas; ☐ institution/knowledge study |
| `ISL-I08` Muslim societies, c. 1500–1800 | `ISL-I07`, `HST-I02` | 1. Ottoman; 2. Safavid; 3. Mughal; 4. Africa/Indian Ocean/Southeast Asia; 5. law, trade, culture, reform | Compare imperial/regional institutions; trace networks; analyze confessional change | Early modern Muslim societies were not simply in uniform decline | ☐ Compare three societies; ☐ analyze connected source set |
| `ISL-I09` Colonialism, reform, revival, states, movements, contemporary societies | `ISL-I08`, `HST-I05` | 1. Colonial transformations; 2. reform/modernism/revival; 3. nationalism/state law; 4. movements/migration; 5. contemporary diversity/media | Explain modern transformations; distinguish movements; connect global/local causes | “Islamism,” reform, traditionalism, and Muslim politics are not interchangeable | ☐ Comparative modern-history essay; ☐ movement/source analysis |
| `ISL-A01` Advanced legal reasoning and contemporary applications | `ISL-I04`, `LAW-I07` | 1. Advanced usul/ijtihad; 2. legal disagreement/fatwa; 3. courts/state/codification; 4. finance/bioethics/minorities; 5. authority/institutions | Reconstruct advanced reasoning; compare premodern/state contexts; formulate bounded contemporary analysis | Invoking objectives alone does not remove textual, juristic, or institutional constraints | ☐ Advanced case memorandum; ☐ defend methodology |
| `ISL-A02` Advanced tafsir, hadith, theology, philosophy, intellectual history | `ISL-I02`, `ISL-I03`, `ISL-I05` | 1. Manuscript/commentary traditions; 2. advanced exegetical disputes; 3. hadith legal/theological use; 4. kalam/falsafa exchanges; 5. reception/intellectual networks | Conduct source-critical intellectual history; compare commentary layers; integrate disciplines | Later commentaries are not mere repetition without innovation | ☐ Research paper with primary sources; ☐ oral text defense |
| `ISL-A03` Islam, science, economics, politics, gender, pluralism, modern ethics | `ISL-I09`, `PHI-I05`, `ECO-I03`, `POL-I03` | 1. Modern knowledge/science; 2. economy/finance; 3. state/democracy; 4. gender/family; 5. pluralism/bio/tech/environment | Separate descriptive/normative claims; compare modern arguments; use interdisciplinary evidence | Adding “Islamic” to a modern institution does not establish continuity or legitimacy | ☐ Issue dossier across positions; ☐ defend evidence/normative method |
| `ISL-A04` Islamic art, architecture, literature, manuscript, material culture | `ISL-I07`, `ART-I03`, `LIT-I04` | 1. Mosque/city; 2. calligraphy/manuscript; 3. ornament/object; 4. Arabic/Persian/Turkic and other literatures; 5. patronage/museum/heritage | Interpret form/function/context; compare regions; analyze manuscripts/material evidence | Aniconism is not a uniform ban on all figural representation | ☐ Object/building analysis; ☐ curated material-culture exhibit |
| `ISL-A05` Historiography, orientalism, decolonial critique, archives, digital studies | `ISL-I09`, `HST-A01`, `RSH-A04` | 1. Muslim historiography; 2. orientalism/philology; 3. colonial archives; 4. decolonial/gender/social histories; 5. digital corpora/method ethics | Critique knowledge production; use archives reflexively; assess digital bias | Critiquing orientalism does not license rejecting all philology or external scholarship | ☐ Historiographic intervention; ☐ audit archive/digital corpus |
| `ISL-E01` Primary-source research in an Islamic discipline | `ISL-I01`, `ISL-A02`, `RSH-E01` | 1. Advanced language/text; 2. manuscript/edition; 3. disciplinary method; 4. original argument; 5. scholarly/community dissemination | Produce original primary-source research; edit/translate accurately; locate normative status | Translation or transcription alone is not yet an analytical contribution | ☐ Complete thesis/critical text; ☐ pass language/method defense |
