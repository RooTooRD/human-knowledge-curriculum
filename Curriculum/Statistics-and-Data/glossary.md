# Statistics and Data Glossary

These are curriculum-scoped working definitions. They identify how a term is used
in its unit and the operation mastery requires; consult the unit’s advanced
reference for competing, historical, or specialist definitions.

| Unit | Terms |
|---|---|
| `STA-B01` | **Data:** Within Data, variables, cases, measurement, and data-generating processes, the organizing beginner idea that connects questions, cases, variables, values with categorical/quantitative and discrete/continuous data; in this curriculum it supports the ability to define an analysis-ready data structure.<br>**variables:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through population, process, and data-generating mechanism; mastery includes the ability to classify variables. |
| `STA-B02` | **Sampling:** Within Sampling, bias, missingness, observational studies, and experiments, the organizing beginner idea that connects target population and frame with random, stratified, cluster, convenience samples; in this curriculum it supports the ability to evaluate sample/design fit.<br>**bias:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through bias and generalization; mastery includes the ability to identify selection and missing-data threats. |
| `STA-B04` | **Distributions:** Within Distributions, summary statistics, visualization, and robust description, the organizing beginner idea that connects frequency and distribution shape with center, spread, quantiles; in this curriculum it supports the ability to select/compute summaries and plots.<br>**summary statistics:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through multivariate comparison; mastery includes the ability to describe shape and association without causal language. |
| `STA-B03` | **Chance:** Within Chance, conditional probability, independence, and Bayes' rule, the organizing beginner idea that connects sample spaces and events with addition/multiplication rules; in this curriculum it supports the ability to compute probabilities.<br>**conditional probability:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through trees, tables, and simulation; mastery includes the ability to translate among representations. |
| `STA-I01` | **Random variables:** Within Random variables, expectation, variance, covariance, and common distributions, the organizing intermediate idea that connects discrete/continuous random variables with CDF, density, mass; in this curriculum it supports the ability to derive moments.<br>**expectation:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through transformations and joint distributions; mastery includes the ability to choose distributions from assumptions. |
| `STA-I02` | **Laws of large numbers:** Within Laws of large numbers, central limit theorem, and simulation, the organizing intermediate idea that connects sampling distributions with LLN and stabilization; in this curriculum it supports the ability to explain repeated-sampling behavior.<br>**central limit theorem:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through approximation conditions and convergence diagnostics; mastery includes the ability to use CLT appropriately. |
| `STA-I03` | **Estimation:** Within Estimation, uncertainty intervals, testing, effect size, and power, the organizing intermediate idea that connects estimands and estimators with bias, variance, consistency; in this curriculum it supports the ability to conduct and interpret inference.<br>**uncertainty intervals:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through effect size, multiplicity, power, and sample size; mastery includes the ability to separate practical/statistical significance. |
| `STA-I04` | **Linear:** Within Linear, generalized linear, and multilevel regression, the organizing intermediate idea that connects linear model and least squares with diagnostics, transformations, interactions; in this curriculum it supports the ability to fit, diagnose, and interpret regression.<br>**generalized linear:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through prediction versus explanation; mastery includes the ability to encode interactions. |
| `STA-I05` | **Bayesian modeling:** Within Bayesian modeling, priors, likelihoods, posteriors, and prediction, the organizing intermediate idea that connects prior, likelihood, posterior with conjugacy and computation; in this curriculum it supports the ability to build/update Bayesian models.<br>**priors:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through model checking, sensitivity, and decision; mastery includes the ability to interpret posterior quantities. |
| `STA-I06` | **Data cleaning:** Within Data cleaning, provenance, formats, querying, and reproducible analysis, the organizing intermediate idea that connects tidy/relational structures and schemas with parsing, types, joins, reshape; in this curriculum it supports the ability to build an auditable cleaning pipeline.<br>**provenance:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through scripts, environments, lineage, and reports; mastery includes the ability to query/join safely. |
| `STA-A01` | **Causal inference:** Within Causal inference: potential outcomes, graphs, identification, and designs, the organizing advanced idea that connects counterfactuals and estimands with DAGs, backdoors, colliders; in this curriculum it supports the ability to state causal assumptions.<br>**potential outcomes:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through sensitivity and transport; mastery includes the ability to identify effects. |
| `STA-A02` | **Time series:** Within Time series, spatial, survival, and longitudinal data, the organizing advanced idea that connects dependence and repeated measures with trend, seasonality, ARIMA/state space; in this curriculum it supports the ability to model dependence correctly.<br>**spatial:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through longitudinal mixed models; mastery includes the ability to forecast with uncertainty. |
| `STA-A03` | **Nonparametric:** Within Nonparametric, resampling, robust, and high-dimensional methods, the organizing advanced idea that connects rank and distribution-free methods with bootstrap and permutation; in this curriculum it supports the ability to select assumption-light methods.<br>**resampling:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through multiple testing and high dimension; mastery includes the ability to construct resampling inference. |
| `STA-A04` | **Statistical learning:** Within Statistical learning, regularization, validation, and decision rules, the organizing advanced idea that connects prediction loss and generalization with train/validation/test and cross-validation; in this curriculum it supports the ability to build leakage-free predictive workflows.<br>**regularization:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through calibration, thresholds, decision costs; mastery includes the ability to tune/compare models. |
| `STA-A05` | **Survey:** Within Survey, administrative, sensor, text, image, and network data, the organizing advanced idea that connects survey weights and complex samples with administrative linkage; in this curriculum it supports the ability to adapt data models to collection structure.<br>**administrative:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through network observations and dependence; mastery includes the ability to document linkage/measurement error. |
| `STA-A06` | **Privacy:** Within Privacy, fairness, uncertainty communication, and statistical ethics, the organizing advanced idea that connects confidentiality, reidentification, minimization with disclosure control and differential privacy; in this curriculum it supports the ability to conduct ethical data-risk review.<br>**fairness:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through governance, consent, and harm; mastery includes the ability to calculate/interpret privacy trade-offs. |
| `STA-E01` | **Mathematical statistics theory:** Within Mathematical statistics and asymptotic theory, the organizing expert idea that connects sufficiency, completeness, exponential families with decision theory and optimality; in this curriculum it supports the ability to prove estimator/test properties.<br>**asymptotic theory:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through empirical processes orientation; mastery includes the ability to derive asymptotic distributions. |
| `STA-E02` | **Original statistical methodology collaboration:** Within Original statistical methodology and domain collaboration, the organizing expert idea that connects domain problem and estimand with literature and methodological gap; in this curriculum it supports the ability to co-develop a defensible method.<br>**domain collaboration:** A concept, distinction, or method in this unit, developed through application, communication, and reproducibility; mastery includes the ability to establish properties. |
